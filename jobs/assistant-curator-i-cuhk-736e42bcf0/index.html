Assistant Curator I

Chinese University of Hong Kong

Department
Art Museum
Rank
Other
Position type
Full-time

Duties & Responsibilities

  • Provide strategic leadership for Art and Technology initiatives, including AI architecture for historical document analysis and museum metadata engineering.
  • Oversee multidisciplinary research projects involving data management and collaboration across schools, faculties, museums, and external institutions.
  • Develop project plans, implementation schedules, and quality control procedures to ensure effective governance and timely delivery.
  • Conduct research and evaluation of OCR, computer vision, and AI-assisted workflows for processing calligraphic and historical documents.
  • Manage AI/IT budgets, outsourcing, vendor coordination, and technical environment setup.
  • Facilitate collaboration between researchers, scholars, curators, educators, and technical specialists.
  • Supervise student interns and mentor the Art & Technology task force.

Requirements & Qualifications

  • Master's degree or above in Computer Science, Artificial Intelligence, Library and Information Management, Data Science, Museum Studies, or related discipline.
  • At least 8 years' post-qualification relevant work experience, preferably in arts, information management, AI, or creative industries.
  • Solid foundation in digital humanities, cultural heritage, metadata, AI/OCR workflows, and project coordination.
  • Experience in a supervisory role.
  • Excellent written and verbal communication skills in English and Chinese.
  • Experience making grant applications.
  • Strong interpersonal and problem-solving skills.
  • Knowledge of cursive calligraphy is a strong advantage.

Appointment

  • Full-time — 1-year contract, renewable subject to good performance and mutual agreement.
