Professor / Associate Professor / Assistant Professor in English Language Education

Chinese University of Hong Kong

Department
Department of Curriculum and Instruction
Rank
Associate Professor
Position type
Full-time

Duties & Responsibilities

  • Teach and design courses in English language teaching for primary and secondary school teachers and TESOL professionals.
  • Undertake scholarly research and pursue external competitive research grants.
  • Conduct teaching practicum supervision and supervise doctoral students.
  • Direct and manage programmes within the department.

Requirements & Qualifications

  • Hold excellent academic qualifications including a relevant doctoral degree.
  • Demonstrate strong commitment to excellent teaching.
  • Show a track record of active research and publications.
  • For Professor/Associate Professor level: demonstrate outstanding research, successful grant acquisition, international scholarship impact, and experience supervising research postgraduate students.

Appointment

  • Full-time contract for up to three years initially, with potential for longer-term appointment or substantiation subject to funding and mutual agreement.
