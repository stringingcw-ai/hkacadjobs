Duties & Responsibilities

  • Support marketing and promotion activities across online and offline publication channels.
  • Provide logistics support for programme events and activities.
  • Handle procurement and payment follow-up with vendors.

  • Excellent command of written and spoken English and Chinese, including fluency in Putonghua.
  • Digital marketing knowledge and social media management experience (particularly Chinese Mainland platforms) is advantageous.
