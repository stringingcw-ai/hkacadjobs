Duties & Responsibilities

  • Conduct archival research using physical and digitised historical collections in Hong Kong.
  • Retrieve, organise, and plot historical data for a digital history project on children's steamship journeys.
  • Manage digital files and create a searchable data repository.
  • Translate historical documents from Chinese to English.
  • Compile bibliographies and manage bibliographic data using Zotero.
  • Support other duties assigned by the Principal Investigator.

Requirements & Qualifications

  • Master's degree or above in History or related discipline (Senior Research Assistant); doctoral degree may qualify for Research Associate position.
  • Bachelor's degree or above in History or related discipline (Research Assistant).
  • Demonstrated experience conducting independent historical research using physical and digitised repositories.
  • Experience working on digital humanities projects; ArcGIS and Google Maps knowledge advantageous.
  • Interest in maritime history, childhood history, and global history.
  • Excellent written and spoken English; excellent written Chinese; other Asian languages advantageous.
  • Strong analytical, communication, organisational, and problem-solving skills.

Appointment

  • Full-time, fixed contract for 6 months starting between June and early September 2026; re-appointment subject to mutual agreement.

Key Dates

  • Start date: Between June and early September 2026
  • Interview notification: 8 weeks after application submission
