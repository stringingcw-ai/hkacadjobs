Duties & Responsibilities

  • Provide administrative and clerical support to the Graduate School.
  • Coordinate and organize various events and activities hosted by the Graduate School.
  • Handle student inquiries and maintain communication with postgraduate students.
  • Manage purchases, reimbursements, payments, and financial/budgeting matters.
  • Manage and maintain the Graduate School's website, promotional materials, surveys and reports.
  • Offer assistance to other units within the Graduate School as needed.

Requirements & Qualifications

  • Hold a recognized bachelor's degree.
  • Have at least 1 year of experience in tertiary institutions.
  • Demonstrate good command of written and spoken English and Chinese (Cantonese and Putonghua).
  • Possess strong computer literacy, including proficiency in Chinese word processing.
  • Have good communication and interpersonal skills.
  • Be a team player with ability to quickly learn and adapt.
