Executive Officer II/ Project Co-ordinator II (carrying the job title of ‘Student Development Manager’/ ‘Assistant Student Development Manager’)

Chinese University of Hong Kong

Department
Office of Student Affairs
Rank
Non-Academic
Position type
Full-time

Duties & Responsibilities

  • Plan, organize, and evaluate student programmes and activities to foster student development and engagement.
  • Provide guidance and support to students, respond to enquiries, identify needs, and make referrals to appropriate units.
  • Prepare proposals, reports, publicity materials, and statistical summaries to support service planning.
  • Analyze programme data and feedback to improve services.
  • Liaise and collaborate with faculties, departments, student bodies, and internal/external stakeholders.
  • Handle communication with students and stakeholders, and assist in daily administration of student services.

Requirements & Qualifications

  • Good Bachelor's degree.
  • At least 3 years' post-qualification relevant work experience, preferably in student advising, student services, social services, or youth work.
  • Excellent command of English and Chinese, including Putonghua.
  • Outgoing personality and creativity.
  • Excellent task management, interpersonal, and communication skills.
  • Self-motivated with can-do spirit.
  • Ability to work both in a team and independently.
  • Must submit certificates confirming language requirements and academic qualifications.

Appointment

  • Full-time, 2-year contract with contract-end gratuity; renewable subject to good performance and mutual agreement.
