Duties & Responsibilities

  • Lead financial planning, budgetary control, compliance, and business development initiatives within the Faculty.
  • Support efficient budgeting and forecasting processes aligned with University planning.
  • Ensure compliance with external and internal governance requirements including financial controls and cost allocations.
  • Provide financial analysis to support business growth and evaluate implications of new activities and collaborations.
  • Supervise and coach subordinates while providing operational support to senior executives.
  • Develop the finance team to enhance departmental effectiveness and manage internal customer relationships.

Requirements & Qualifications

  • Bachelor's degree in accounting or related discipline with recognised accounting qualification.
  • Minimum 8 years' professional finance/accounting experience, including 3 years post-qualification.
  • Ability to work with significant independence and sound judgement.
  • Strong understanding of governance and compliance, preferably in public sector or large organisations.
  • Excellent communication and presentation skills in both English and Chinese.
  • Proficiency in MS Office, Oracle financial system, and QlikView business intelligence tools.
  • Innovative mindset with ability to work under pressure and drive change.

Appointment

  • Full-time contract — two-year fixed-term with possibility of renewal subject to funding and performance; includes contract-end gratuity and up to 10% University retirement benefits contribution.
