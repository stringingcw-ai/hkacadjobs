Works Supervisor I/II (Building Services – Plumbing and Drainage) (carrying the job title of ‘Maintenance Officer’)

Chinese University of Hong Kong

Department
Estates Management Office
Rank
Non-Academic
Position type
Full-time

Duties & Responsibilities

  • Supervise alteration and improvement works on plumbing and drainage systems.
  • Plan and lead internal staff for repair and maintenance of P&D systems.
  • Perform regular inspections of building services.
  • Liaise on systems operation regarding energy saving and carbon reduction.
  • Work overtime, shifts, and weekends/public holidays as required.

Requirements & Qualifications

  • Hold a Diploma or Higher Certificate in Mechanical Engineering or Building Services Engineering from a recognised institution.
  • Have at least 3 years' supervisory experience in plumbing, drainage, swimming pool, compressed air and LP gas systems.
  • Prepare reports in both English and Chinese.
  • Possess good computer skills including MS Office and AutoCAD.
  • Demonstrate good interpersonal skills and ability to work under tight schedules.
  • Plumbers' license is advantageous; candidates with 1 year's relevant experience may be considered for Works Supervisor II.

Appointment

  • Full-time contract: 2-year initial contract with contract-end gratuity, renewable subject to good performance and mutual agreement.
