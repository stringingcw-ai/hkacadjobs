Duties & Responsibilities

  • Supervise oral care workshops and seminars.
  • Provide dental screening and emergency services for dental hygiene promotion.
  • Assist in programme design, validation, and quality assurance implementation.
  • Design educational kits to promote oral health care.
  • Supervise Dental Hygienists and Dental Surgery Assistants.
  • Prepare interim and annual performance reports with senior management.

Requirements & Qualifications

  • Hold a recognized degree in Dentistry or relevant professional qualification, preferably with postgraduate qualification.
  • Be registered as a Dentist and hold a valid practicing certificate from the Dental Council.
  • Have at least 5 years of relevant experience.
  • Demonstrate good professional development records and industry linkages.
  • Possess strong administrative, interpersonal, communication and team building skills.
  • Have excellent presentation skills and high proficiency in English and Chinese, preferably including Putonghua.

Appointment

  • Full-time, permanent position at Senior Lecturer rank
