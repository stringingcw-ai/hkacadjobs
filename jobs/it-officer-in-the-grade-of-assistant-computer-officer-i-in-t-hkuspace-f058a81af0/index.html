Duties & Responsibilities

  • Assist the Assistant IT Manager in managing the Web Application Team and overseeing daily operations, enhancements, and support of web applications including admission systems, websites, and AI chatbot.
  • Develop, maintain, enhance, and integrate web application systems and websites.
  • Support system implementation, upgrades, testing, and deployment while maintaining service quality, availability, security, and scalability.
  • Assist in adopting and integrating AI technologies into web applications and supporting AI-enabled solutions development.
  • Troubleshoot web system issues, handle incidents, and liaise with internal teams and external vendors.
  • Support project activities including requirements gathering, documentation, testing, vendor coordination, and implementation.
  • Maintain IT governance and cybersecurity standards, prepare system documentation, and stay current with AI and web technology developments.

Requirements & Qualifications

  • Bachelor's degree in information technology, computer science, or software engineering.
  • Minimum four years of post-qualification experience in full-stack web development.
  • Proficiency in modern frontend technologies including TypeScript, JavaScript, Next.js, HTML5/CSS3.
  • Solid backend development experience in Python and/or PHP with RESTful API design and microservices knowledge.
  • Experience with relational databases such as MySQL, PostgreSQL, or Oracle.
  • Strong problem-solving abilities and effective communication skills to work with cross-functional teams.
  • Willingness to learn new technologies and use AI tools to improve productivity; prior experience with admission systems in higher education is desirable.

Appointment

  • Full-time, 2-year contract (renewable)
