Duties & Responsibilities

  • Lead and manage research projects on haematolymphoid neoplasms and related diseases using clinical samples, cell lines, and in-vivo models.
  • Discover and characterize novel biomarkers and drug targets in lymphoid neoplasms.
  • Participate in collaborative projects on haematolymphoid diseases with partner institutions.

Requirements & Qualifications

  • Ph.D. degree in biomedical sciences or related discipline required.
  • Preferably have experience in cancer biology, cell culture, flow cytometry, and in-vivo models.
  • Demonstrate excellent communication and collaboration skills.
  • Ability to work effectively both independently and as part of a multidisciplinary team.

Appointment

  • Full-time contract for one to two years, with possibility of renewal subject to satisfactory performance and funding availability.
