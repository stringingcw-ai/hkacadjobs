Duties & Responsibilities

  • Support undergraduate courses through assistance with preparation, delivery, and administration.
  • Conduct tutorials and lead in-class discussions.
  • Grade assignments and tests while providing constructive feedback to students.
  • Manage course Moodle platforms and invigilate examinations.
  • Perform additional duties as assigned by the Department, occasionally outside normal office hours.

Requirements & Qualifications

  • Master's Degree or above in Social Sciences or a related discipline.
  • Minimum two years of experience in teaching support within tertiary education.
  • Excellent analytical, problem-solving, and communication skills.
  • Excellent command of written and spoken English.

Appointment

  • One-year temporary term or two-year fixed-term contract with contract-end gratuity and up to 10% University contribution to retirement benefits; renewal subject to satisfactory performance and funding availability.
