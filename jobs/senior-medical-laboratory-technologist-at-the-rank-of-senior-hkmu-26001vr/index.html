Duties & Responsibilities

  • Prepare and develop teaching materials, deliver lectures, tutorials, and laboratory classes.
  • Plan, develop, coordinate and deliver courses in related disciplines.
  • Provide clinical teaching to students and support practicum in hospitals.
  • Supervise undergraduate and postgraduate research projects.
  • Participate in programme development and accreditation exercises.
  • Train and supervise laboratory staff to prepare materials and equipment.
  • Write standard operation procedures for equipment setup and maintenance.
  • Ensure safety and security in laboratories.

Requirements & Qualifications

  • Hold a recognised higher degree in medical laboratory science or related discipline.
  • Possess Part 1 Registration and valid practicing certificate with the Medical Laboratory Technologist Board of HKSAR.
  • Have substantial professional experience in medical laboratory settings, specifically in Anatomical and Cellular Pathology, Clinical Chemistry, Immunology, Haematology and Transfusion.
