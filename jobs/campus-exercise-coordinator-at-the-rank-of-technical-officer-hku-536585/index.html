Duties & Responsibilities

  • Develop, lead and promote the Exercise is Medicine initiative to enhance health and active lifestyles.
  • Design and implement on-campus fitness and health programmes.
  • Assist in fitness and wellness projects.
  • Conduct fitness training sessions.
  • Undertake other duties as assigned.

Requirements & Qualifications

  • Possess a recognized degree, preferably in Sports Management, Sports Science, Exercise Science or equivalent.
  • Have at least 3 years' relevant work experience in event or project coordination or coaching.
  • Demonstrate excellent command of spoken and written English and Chinese (preferably Putonghua).
  • Possess good interpersonal, organizational and communication skills.
  • Be proficient in IT and capable of dealing with users positively and professionally.
  • Be dynamic, proactive, and able to work independently or as part of a team.
  • Fitness training certification (NSCA, ACSM, ASCA) is advantageous but not required.

Appointment

  • Full-time, two-year fixed-term contract with possibility of renewal subject to funding availability and satisfactory performance; includes contract-end gratuity and University contribution to retirement benefits scheme (up to 10% of basic salary); highly competitive salary commensurate with qualifications and experience; annual leave and medical benefits provided.

Key Dates

  • Start date: As soon as possible
  • Review commencement date: July 1, 2026
  • Review end date: July 15, 2026
