Duties & Responsibilities

  • Conduct structured field observations across multiple sites in Hong Kong.
  • Deploy, maintain, and retrieve camera traps at research locations.
  • Extract, catalogue, and manage large volumes of camera-trap image data.
  • Record behavioural data using interval sampling and systematic observation methods.
  • Perform data entry, cleaning, and database management to maintain data integrity.
  • Assist in preparing research reports, manuscripts, grant materials, and presentations.
  • Undertake other duties as assigned by the Principal Investigator.

Requirements & Qualifications

  • Achieve Level 2 or above in five HKDSE subjects including Chinese Language, English Language, and Mathematics (or equivalent).
  • Possess a degree in Ecology, Geography, Conservation Science, Zoology, Environmental Science, or related field (preferred).
  • Have demonstrated experience in field-based wildlife research.
  • Show experience conducting systematic behavioural observations such as scan sampling and focal sampling.
  • Be familiar with camera-trap deployment and ecological data collection methods.
