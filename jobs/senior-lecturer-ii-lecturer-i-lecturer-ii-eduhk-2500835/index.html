Duties & Responsibilities

  • Teach courses in Chinese Foreign Affairs and International Relations at tertiary level.
  • Develop new courses addressing China's role in global governance and regional affairs.
  • Provide academic advising and mentorship to support student intellectual and professional development.
  • Contribute to fostering a dynamic scholarly environment within the Academy.
  • Travel outside Hong Kong and undertake work as assigned by the University.

Requirements & Qualifications

  • Hold a Master's degree in Chinese Foreign Affairs, International Relations, or related discipline with minimum 8 years of post-qualification teaching experience, OR a Doctoral degree with at least 3 years of post-qualification teaching or professional experience.
  • Demonstrate at least two years of university-level teaching experience and subject expertise.
  • Show pedagogical competence in Chinese Foreign Affairs and International Relations.
  • Be capable of developing courses on China's evolving global role and related topics.
  • Have familiarity with interdisciplinary approaches and ability to contextualise China's foreign policy within global trends.
