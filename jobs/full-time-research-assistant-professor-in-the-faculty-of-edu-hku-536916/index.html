Duties & Responsibilities

  • Lead the scientific design, oversight, and execution of a large-scale hearing Randomized Controlled Trial (RCT).
  • Develop and refine scientific protocols for hearing screening, device fitting, and outcome measurement.
  • Manage research staff, data collection, analysis, and ensure rigorous data integrity and compliance with international standards.
  • Prepare test materials, conduct data analysis using advanced statistical software, and provide staff training on hearing-related issues.
  • Write specifications and assist with hearing device tendering processes.
  • Document and report research findings through conference presentations and peer-reviewed publications.

Requirements & Qualifications

  • PhD or equivalent doctorate in audiology, hearing science, or a related field.
  • Proven experience in managing research projects and complex, multi-site clinical trials.
  • Strong publication record in auditory research and keen interest in hearing screening or device fitting.
  • Experience supervising interdisciplinary teams.
  • Fluent English communication skills and ability to understand Chinese, preferably Cantonese.

Appointment

  • Full-time, three-year fixed-term contract (1 November 2026 to 31 October 2029).

  • Application deadline: 31 August 2026 (or until post is filled, whichever is earlier)
  • Appointment commencement: 1 November 2026
  • Appointment end: 31 October 2029
