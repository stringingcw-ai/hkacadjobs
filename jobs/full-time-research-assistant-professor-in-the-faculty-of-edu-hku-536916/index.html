Duties & Responsibilities

  • Lead scientific design, oversight, and execution of a large-scale hearing randomized controlled trial (RCT).
  • Develop and refine scientific protocols for hearing screening, device fitting, and outcome measurement.
  • Manage research staff, data collection, analysis, and ensure compliance with international standards.
  • Prepare test materials, conduct data analysis, and provide staff training on hearing-related issues.
  • Assist the Principal Investigator with device tendering and develop intervention manuals for primary healthcare settings.
  • Document and report findings through conferences and peer-reviewed publications.

Requirements & Qualifications

  • PhD or equivalent doctorate in audiology, hearing science, or related field.
  • Proven experience managing research projects with a strong publication record in auditory research.
  • Experience managing complex, multi-site clinical trials and supervising interdisciplinary teams.
  • Advanced proficiency in statistical software (SPSS, STATA, R).
  • Strong English communication skills and ability to understand Chinese, preferably Cantonese.
  • Demonstrated interest in hearing screening and/or hearing device fitting.

Appointment

  • Full-time, fixed-term contract for 3 years
