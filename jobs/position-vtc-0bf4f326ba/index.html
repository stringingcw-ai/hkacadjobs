Duties & Responsibilities

  • Execute teaching, student assessment, and maintenance of training facilities and equipment.
  • Provide administrative support for curriculum implementation and assist in developing teaching materials.
  • Perform student management duties including recruitment, career guidance, and course promotion.
  • Implement quality assurance policies and execute skills testing work.
  • Maintain and strengthen networks with industry and relevant associations.

Requirements & Qualifications

  • Hold a relevant higher diploma or equivalent qualification with three years of industry and/or teaching experience.
  • Possess a valid registered electrical engineer registration certificate (Grade A or above).
  • Demonstrate proficiency in written and spoken Chinese and English, and fluent Cantonese.
  • Show familiarity with general computer applications and pass a skills test.
  • Preference given to applicants with plumber's license, fire safety installation contractor registration, or CIC skills testing certificates.
