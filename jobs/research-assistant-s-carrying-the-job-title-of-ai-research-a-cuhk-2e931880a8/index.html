Research Assistant(s) (carrying the job title of 'AI Research Assistant')

Chinese University of Hong Kong

Department
Department of Mathematics
Rank
Research Assistant/Associate
Position type
Full-time

Duties & Responsibilities

  • Conduct literature reviews on vision-language models, multimodal fusion, and self-supervised learning paradigms.
  • Implement, fine-tune, and deploy large-scale pretrained models for perception, diagnostics, and decision-making tasks.
  • Develop and optimize training pipelines for multimodal data with domain-specific physical constraints.
  • Design and experiment with reinforcement learning frameworks for autonomous decision-making in industrial environments.
  • Enhance model interpretability through mathematical analysis and visualization of attention mechanisms and decision pathways.
  • Optimize system architecture for edge-cloud deployment, including model compression and inference acceleration.
  • Prepare technical documentation, experiment reports, and presentations for academic and industrial stakeholders.

Requirements & Qualifications

  • Bachelor's or Master's degree in AI, Computer Science, Data Science, Electrical Engineering, Applied Mathematics, or related field.
  • Hands-on experience with deep learning frameworks (PyTorch/TensorFlow/Llama-Factory) and large model fine-tuning.
  • Practical exposure to multimodal architectures (Vision Transformers, VLMs) and reinforcement learning algorithms.
  • Familiarity with training/inference optimization techniques such as distributed training, model pruning, and quantization.
  • Basic understanding of mathematical concepts in deep learning (optimization, probability, linear algebra) and interest in interpretability.
  • Self-driven, collaborative, and keen to work in an interdisciplinary research environment.

Appointment

  • Contract — up to two years (renewable)
