Duties & Responsibilities

  • Perform course and general administration and office duties for various units.
  • Provide counter services and handle general and telephone enquiries.
  • Conduct English and Chinese word processing and data input.
  • Liaise with internal and external parties regarding admissions, timetabling, course selection, and examinations.
  • Maintain student and staff records and manage filing systems.
  • Undertake overtime work as required.

Requirements & Qualifications

  • Achieve Level 2 or equivalent in five subjects in HKDSEE (or HKCEE Grade E equivalent).
  • Demonstrate Level 2 proficiency in Chinese and English Language.
  • Possess fluency in spoken Cantonese and Putonghua with strong communication skills.
  • Have proficiency in Microsoft Office and Chinese word processing.
  • Be energetic, able to work independently, and willing to take on responsibilities.
  • Associate Degree or Higher Diploma with tertiary education experience preferred.

Appointment

  • Contract — 2 years with possibility of renewal subject to review.
