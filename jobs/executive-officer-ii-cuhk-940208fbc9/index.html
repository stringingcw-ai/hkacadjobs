Executive Officer II

Chinese University of Hong Kong

Department
Department of Biomedical Engineering
Rank
Non-Academic
Position type
Full-time

Duties & Responsibilities

  • Deliver comprehensive administrative support to the Chairperson and Department.
  • Manage office administration, budgeting, financial, staffing and personnel matters.
  • Provide secretarial support to committees and meetings.
  • Oversee administrative matters for undergraduate and postgraduate academic programmes.
  • Manage student affairs and supervise clerical and junior staff.
  • Liaise with internal and external parties to strengthen departmental visibility and partnerships.
  • Coordinate department-level initiatives and activities.

Requirements & Qualifications

  • Bachelor's degree or higher.
  • At least 5 years' post-qualification executive experience, preferably in tertiary institutions.
  • Excellent command of written and spoken English and Chinese (Cantonese and Putonghua).
  • Strong analytical, organisational, interpersonal and communication skills.
  • Proficiency in computer applications.
  • Ability to work independently and under pressure.
  • Strong sense of responsibility, integrity, and self-motivation.
  • Must submit certificates proving language requirements and academic qualifications.

Appointment

  • Full-time, 2-year contract with contract-end gratuity, renewable subject to good performance and mutual agreement.
