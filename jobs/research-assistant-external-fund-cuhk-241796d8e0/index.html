Research Assistant (External Fund)

Chinese University of Hong Kong

Department
The Nethersole School of Nursing
Rank
Research Assistant/Associate
Position type
Full-time

Duties & Responsibilities

  • Support participant screening, recruitment, and informed consent processes.
  • Administer questionnaires and coordinate study assessments.
  • Assist with study coordination and project administration for AI-supported mental health research.
  • Manage and quality-check research data including digital and conversational platforms.
  • Prepare progress reports, manuscripts, and research documents.
  • Perform additional research and logistical support as assigned.
  • Occasional irregular hours or weekend work may be required.

Requirements & Qualifications

  • Bachelor's degree or above in Computer Science, Data Science, Health Informatics, Psychology, Nursing, or related discipline.
  • At least two years' full-time experience developing or supporting AI applications, preferably in healthcare or mental health.
  • Proficiency in Python and familiarity with ML/LLM tools and cloud AI services (e.g., Azure OpenAI).
  • Strong interest in applying AI to mental health or healthcare.
  • Experience in conversational agents, prompt engineering, or usability testing for digital health platforms is advantageous.
  • CV and academic certificates must be submitted with application.

Appointment

  • Contract — one year, renewable subject to good performance and mutual agreement.
