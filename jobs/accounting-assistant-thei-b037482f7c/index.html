Duties & Responsibilities

  • Handle daily accounting operations including processing payments, issuing receipts, and updating records.
  • Assist in preparing management reports and statistics.
  • Assist in budget preparation and maintain accounting and asset registers.
  • Handle enquiries from operating units and provide timely accounting information.
  • Perform other ad hoc tasks as assigned.

Requirements & Qualifications

  • Hong Kong Diploma of Secondary Education (HKDSE) Level 2 or above in 5 subjects including English, Chinese and Mathematics, or equivalent qualification.
  • Proficiency in MS Word, MS Excel, MS PowerPoint, and Chinese word processing.
  • Good communication and interpersonal skills.
  • Good command of both written and spoken English and Chinese.
  • Ability to work independently and as a team player.
  • Relevant accounting experience preferred.

Appointment

  • Full-time, rank of Executive Assistant II; may require work at different sites, irregular hours, shifts, and Saturday/Sunday work.
