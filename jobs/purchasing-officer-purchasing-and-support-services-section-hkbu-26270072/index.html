Duties & Responsibilities

  • Process orders, manage tenders and quotations, and source goods and services.
  • Coordinate with user departments to finalize tender specifications and fulfill purchase requisitions.
  • Conduct price analysis and prepare tender reports.
  • Manage supplier relationships, insurance matters, car pool services, and warehouse operations.
  • Use the E-tender System and Oracle System to manage procurement processes.
  • Arrange site visits and tender interviews to communicate requirements to suppliers and contractors.

Requirements & Qualifications

  • Bachelor's degree in Business Administration, Supply Chain Management, Procurement, or related field.
  • Preferably five years of relevant work experience.
  • Strong written and spoken English and Chinese language skills.
  • Knowledge of Oracle Financials System and procurement management certification are advantageous.

Appointment

  • Fixed-term contract of two years; re-appointment subject to mutual agreement and funding availability.

Key Dates

  • Review of applications: Ongoing until position is filled.
  • Interview notification deadline: 8 weeks after application submission.
