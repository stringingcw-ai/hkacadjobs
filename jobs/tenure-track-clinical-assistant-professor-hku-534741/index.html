Duties & Responsibilities

  • Provide clinical services at the University's teaching hospitals.

  • Possess a medical qualification registrable with the Hong Kong Medical Council.
  • Hold postgraduate professional qualifications in clinical microbiology and infectious diseases.
  • Demonstrate excellence in teaching with commitment to its development.
  • Show potential for conducting independent research with a track record of publications.
  • Have broad knowledge and clinical experience in clinical microbiology and infectious diseases.

Appointment
