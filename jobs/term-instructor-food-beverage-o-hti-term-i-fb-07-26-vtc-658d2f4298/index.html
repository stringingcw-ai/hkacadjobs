Term Instructor (Food & Beverage)(O/HTI/Term I(FB)/07/26)

Rank

Position type

Application deadline
12 Aug 2026

Duties & Responsibilities

  • Conduct classes and practical training in food and beverage service.
  • Assist in curriculum development and support training programme operations.
  • Support day-to-day operations of training restaurants and The T Hotel food and beverage service.
  • Train potential competitors for the WorldSkills Competition – Restaurant Service trade.
  • Maintain department records and assist with student supervision, recruitment, and placement.
  • Provide stewarding services and assist in equipment maintenance.

Requirements & Qualifications

  • Hold a recognised higher diploma in Hotel and/or Catering Management or equivalent, or have 8 years of relevant work/teaching experience.
  • Have at least 3 years' relevant work or teaching experience in the hotel/catering industry.
  • Possess good knowledge of trade-specific terminologies and proficiency in spoken and written English and Chinese.
  • Have knowledge of Point of Sale (POS), Table Management System (TMS), and related computer applications.
  • Be able to pass a trade test.
  • Professional memberships, supervisory experience, upscale dining operations experience, or skills competition experience are advantageous.
