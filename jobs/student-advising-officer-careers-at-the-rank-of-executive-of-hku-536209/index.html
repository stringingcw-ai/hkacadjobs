Duties & Responsibilities

  • Design and implement career programmes to enhance student employability and industry readiness.
  • Collaborate with internal teams, employers, and stakeholders to deliver career development initiatives.
  • Conduct market research and analyse employment trends in Hong Kong, the Greater Bay Area, and beyond.
  • Coordinate employer engagement activities, networking events, and recruitment partnerships.
  • Provide career coaching to students and graduates and support the development of career communities.

Requirements & Qualifications

  • Hold a university degree with at least five years of work experience in project management, career services, or talent development.
  • Demonstrate proven experience in designing and delivering training programmes with evaluation skills.
  • Possess strong organisational, communication, interpersonal, and problem-solving abilities.
  • Show proactive, independent, and results-oriented approach with ability to multitask under pressure.
  • Have excellent knowledge of local employment markets and global industry trends.
  • Demonstrate fluent written and spoken English and Chinese (including Putonghua).
  • Show proficiency in Microsoft Office; webpage design and social media experience is advantageous.

Appointment

  • Full-time, two-year fixed-term contract with end-of-contract gratuity and university retirement benefit contribution (up to 10% of basic salary).
