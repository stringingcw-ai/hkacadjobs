Duties & Responsibilities

  • Develop and implement programmes to help non-local students adapt to local academic, social, and cultural environments.
  • Organize orientation activities, cultural exchange events, and social integration programmes.
  • Provide assistance and guidance to students facing adjustment, well-being, or crisis challenges.
  • Plan and execute publicity strategies across digital and social media platforms to promote services and events.
  • Create engaging multimedia content tailored to diverse student communities.
  • Revamp the website to improve accessibility and user experience for non-local students.
  • Recruit, train, and manage student ambassadors to support programme delivery.
  • Collaborate with internal units and external partners to strengthen support networks.

Requirements & Qualifications

  • Bachelor's degree with 3–5 years of relevant post-qualification administrative experience.
  • Preferably experienced in event management, marketing, multimedia publicity, or tertiary education.
  • Strong communication skills and fluency in Putonghua and English.
  • Familiarity with social media platforms popular among non-local students.
  • Proficiency in publicity, digital marketing, and multimedia content creation.
  • Genuine passion for student support, cultural integration, and proactive engagement.
  • Strong organizational and interpersonal skills.

Appointment

  • Full-time, two-year fixed-term contract with possibility of renewal; contract-end gratuity and University contribution to retirement benefits scheme totalling up to 10% of basic salary included.

Key Dates

  • Application Review Commencement: 2 weeks from posting date.
  • Application Deadline: July 15, 2026 (or until post is filled, whichever is earlier).
