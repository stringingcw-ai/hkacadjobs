Duties & Responsibilities

  • Coordinate and manage logistics for the School's 70th Anniversary celebratory events scheduled for 2027.
  • Support the Anniversary Working Group and Gala Dinner Committee by liaising with internal units, alumni leaders, and external partners.
  • Develop and implement branding and publicity initiatives including commemorative logos, taglines, and promotional materials.
  • Serve as liaison between stakeholders to ensure smooth communication across all anniversary projects.
  • Provide administrative support to manage project timelines, resources, and parallel institutional priorities.

Requirements & Qualifications

  • Bachelor's degree with at least 4 years of experience in event organisation, project coordination, or committee support, preferably in tertiary institutions or large organisations.
  • Proven experience organising committee meetings, including logistics, documentation, and minutes taking.
  • Demonstrated experience organising events and hospitality arrangements with venues, guests, alumni, and service providers.
  • Ability to support large-scale events with multiple stakeholders and tight timelines.
  • Mature, self-motivated, well-organised working style with ability to manage multiple tasks under pressure.
  • Strong documentation, presentation, and writing skills with fluent written and spoken English and Chinese.
  • Proficiency in common office applications and digital coordination tools; AI tool experience is advantageous.
