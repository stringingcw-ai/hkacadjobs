Duties & Responsibilities

  • Recruit research participants from the community for a mental health and healthy ageing study.
  • Obtain consent documents and administer surveys for data collection.
  • Conduct semi-structured interviews and transcribe audio recordings.
  • Perform quantitative data analysis using SPSS and preliminary qualitative thematic analysis.
  • Work at the university and visit NGO centres as required.
  • Complete other duties assigned by the Principal Investigator.

Requirements & Qualifications

  • Hold a recognized bachelor's degree or above in Nursing, health science, or a related discipline.
  • Have 1-2 years of research experience with knowledge of quantitative and qualitative methods.
  • Possess prior experience conducting statistical analysis and thematic analysis.
  • Demonstrate good interpersonal and communication skills.
  • Be able to work independently.
