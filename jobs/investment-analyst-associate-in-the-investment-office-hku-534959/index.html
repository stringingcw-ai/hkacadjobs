Duties & Responsibilities

  • Support investment team in managing diversified portfolios across multiple asset classes and geographies.
  • Conduct research, due diligence, and analysis on asset classes, strategies, sectors, and potential managers.
  • Prepare investment summaries, market updates, and performance analyses for internal stakeholders.
  • Assist in identifying investment opportunities through database landscaping and industry networks.
  • Participate in due diligence meetings and draft investment memos.
  • Support quantitative and risk analysis, including model maintenance and factor analysis.
  • Monitor existing manager performance through fund reports and manager meetings.
  • Collaborate with investment operations team on trade execution, subscriptions, and redemptions.
  • Liaise with legal counsel on documentation including applications, side letters, and NDAs.

Requirements & Qualifications

  • Bachelor's degree in finance, accounting, economics, or related field required.
  • Advanced degree or CFA designation preferred.
  • 2-4 years of experience in portfolio management or investment operations.
  • Experience in endowment, foundation, pension fund, or asset management preferred.
  • Knowledge of private and public market investments and fund operations.
