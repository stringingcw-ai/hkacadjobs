Project Assistant II

Chinese University of Hong Kong

Department
School of Accountancy
Rank
Non-Academic
Position type
Full-time

Duties & Responsibilities

  • Provide clerical support to the Master of Accountancy Programme for admission, academic, student, and alumni matters.
  • Organize logistical support for programme events, student activities, and alumni functions.
  • Handle enquiries from students and the public via phone, email, WeChat, and WhatsApp.
  • Scan and electronically archive documents.
  • Manage procurement, invoices, payments, and maintain inventory records.
  • Oversee office supplies and coordinate facilities maintenance.
  • Assist in daily office operations and perform other assigned duties.

Requirements & Qualifications

  • Completed secondary education, preferably with post-secondary qualification(s).
  • Grade E/Level 2 or above in at least 5 HKCEE subjects (including Chinese, English, and Mathematics) or equivalent HKDSE results.
  • Proficiency in word-processing and MS Office applications.
  • Good command of written and spoken English and Chinese, including Putonghua.
  • Strong interpersonal and communication skills.
  • Strong sense of responsibility and positive work attitude with team spirit.
  • Must submit copies of HKCEE/HKDSE certificates proving language and academic qualifications.

Appointment

  • Contract — up to 2 years (renewable subject to good performance and mutual agreement).
