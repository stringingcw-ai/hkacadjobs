Duties & Responsibilities

  • Establish and lead a state-of-the-art deep proteomics and interactomics capability centered on proteome-wide crosslinking mass spectrometry (XL-MS).
  • Define and oversee purchase, installation and commissioning of mass spectrometry instrumentation, sample-preparation infrastructure and computing resources.
  • Develop, optimize and set up workflows for quantitative proteomics, crosslinking MS, spatial proteomics and protein-protein interaction mapping with associated data-analysis pipelines.
  • Develop new methods in proteomics and XL-MS and publish them in leading international journals.
  • Plan and run collaborative research projects with faculty across the institution in areas including membrane trafficking, autophagy, neurobiology, and stem cell research.
  • Train and supervise research staff, postdoctoral fellows and research students in proteomics and XL-MS methods.

Requirements & Qualifications

  • PhD in biochemistry, analytical chemistry, molecular biology or related field with at least 10 years of postdoctoral and independent research experience.
  • Internationally recognized expertise in developing crosslinking mass spectrometry methods covering crosslinker chemistry, sample preparation, MS acquisition and/or computational analysis.
  • Proven record of high-impact methods development publications as first and senior/corresponding author in leading journals.
  • Extensive hands-on experience with high-resolution Orbitrap-class mass spectrometers, nano-LC systems and large-scale proteomic data analysis.
  • Experience independently leading a research group and/or managing a mass spectrometry or proteomics core facility including instrument and workflow setup.
  • Record of successful interdisciplinary collaborations applying proteomics to cell biology, structural biology or neuroscience questions.
