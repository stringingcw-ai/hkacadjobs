Technical Assistant (Field Data Collection) (several posts)

Social Sciences Research Centre (30700)
Rank
Other
Position type

Duties & Responsibilities

  • Travel to designated survey sites to conduct in-person, door-to-door, or telephone surveys with respondents.
  • Follow established survey protocols, sampling methodologies, and screening processes to ensure valid and unbiased data collection.
  • Create a safe, supportive environment for respondents and handle questions or refusals professionally and empathetically.
  • Maintain strict confidentiality of all respondent information and collected data.
  • Work flexible hours including evenings and weekends to accommodate respondent schedules.
  • Use mobile devices and basic data entry applications to record survey information accurately.

Requirements & Qualifications

  • Excellent verbal communication and interpersonal skills with ability to navigate deeply personal conversations.
  • High accuracy in listening, following complex routing protocols, and recording information.
  • Ability to remain emotionally resilient while hearing difficult accounts while maintaining professional boundaries.
  • Comfortable using tablets, smartphones, and basic data entry applications.
  • Previous experience in counselling, social work, victim advocacy, healthcare, or research with vulnerable populations is advantageous.

Appointment

  • Temporary contract — 3 months
  • Competitive hourly rate commensurate with qualifications and experience
  • Mandatory 5% MPF contribution in accordance with Hong Kong regulations
  • Project-based appointment; future opportunities may be available based on satisfactory performance

Key Dates

  • Commencement: As soon as possible
  • Application review: Starts as soon as possible and continues until posts are filled
