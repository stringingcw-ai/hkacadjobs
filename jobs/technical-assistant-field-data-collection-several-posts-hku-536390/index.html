Duties & Responsibilities

  • Travel to designated survey sites and conduct surveys via in-person, door-to-door, or telephone methods.
  • Adhere strictly to survey protocols, sampling methodologies, and screening processes for valid data collection.
  • Create a safe, supportive environment for respondents and handle objections professionally with empathy.
  • Maintain strict confidentiality and secure handling of all respondent information and collected data.
  • Work flexible hours including evenings and weekends to accommodate respondent schedules.
  • Use mobile devices and basic data entry applications to record survey information accurately.

Requirements & Qualifications

  • Excellent verbal communication and interpersonal skills with comfort in deeply personal conversations.
  • Ability to listen to emotionally difficult accounts while maintaining professional boundaries.
  • High accuracy in listening, following complex routing protocols, and recording information.
  • Comfortable using tablets, smartphones, and basic data entry applications.
  • Previous experience in counselling, social work, victim advocacy, healthcare, or research with vulnerable populations is advantageous.

Appointment

  • Temporary contract position for 3 months, commencing as soon as possible.
  • Competitive hourly rate commensurate with qualifications and experience.
  • Mandatory 5% MPF contribution in accordance with Hong Kong regulations.
  • Project-based appointment; future data collection opportunities may be available upon satisfactory performance.

Key Dates

  • Application review start: As soon as possible and continues until posts are filled.
  • Appointment commencement: As soon as possible.
  • Contract duration: 3 months.
