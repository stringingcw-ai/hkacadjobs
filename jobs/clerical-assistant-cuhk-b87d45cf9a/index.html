Clerical Assistant

Chinese University of Hong Kong

Department
School of Chinese Medicine
Rank
Non-Academic
Position type
Full-time

Duties & Responsibilities

  • Provide reception and payment handling services at the clinics.
  • Assist clinical staff with acupuncture and bone-setting treatments and patient care during consultations.
  • Maintain clinic equipment and facilities.
  • Assist in preparing special events for the clinic.
  • Perform other assigned tasks as needed.

Requirements & Qualifications

  • Completed secondary education with Grade E/Level 2 or above in at least 5 HKCEE subjects (including Chinese and English) or equivalent HKDSE results.
  • Fast and accurate typing skills with strong computer proficiency.
  • Effective command of English, Chinese, and Putonghua.
  • Polite, honest, responsible attitude with punctuality and ability to work independently and as part of a team.
  • Prior clinic or hospital experience is advantageous; clinic front desk experience is highly preferred.
  • Electronic medical systems experience or Chinese Medicine Clinic certification is advantageous.

Appointment

  • Full-time, 1-year contract commencing as soon as possible, renewable subject to good performance and mutual agreement.
