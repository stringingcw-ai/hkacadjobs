Duties & Responsibilities

  • Provide executive and administrative support to the Dean's office, managing schedules, preparing briefing materials, and coordinating follow-up actions.
  • Serve as liaison between the SCA, project teams, University offices, and external partners to facilitate communication and collaboration.
  • Develop and maintain strategic partnerships to enhance the School's profile through stakeholder engagement and campus visits.
  • Support daily operations and prepare reports and secretarial services for internal and external parties.

Requirements & Qualifications

  • Bachelor's degree with minimum 7 years of relevant work experience, preferably in education, arts, or cultural sectors.
  • Strong administrative and organisational skills with ability to support senior management and prepare professional reports and presentations.
  • Excellent written and spoken English and Chinese, including Putonghua.
  • Strong interpersonal, communication, and problem-solving skills with high professionalism and attention to detail.
  • Proficiency in Microsoft Word, Excel, PowerPoint, and Chinese Word.

Appointment

  • Fixed-term contract for up to 2 years, with re-appointment subject to mutual agreement.

  • Interview notification deadline: 8 weeks after application submission.
