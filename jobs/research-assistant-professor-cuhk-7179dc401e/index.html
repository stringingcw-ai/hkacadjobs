Duties & Responsibilities

  • Drive forward a research agenda related to the School's strategic areas in environmental studies.
  • Develop research collaborations within the University and beyond.

  • Demonstrate strong research and practical record in climate impact assessment and urban resilience.
  • Possess excellent proposal writing, communication, and interpersonal skills.
  • Have experience in research grant applications and project management (preferred).
