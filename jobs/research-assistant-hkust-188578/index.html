Duties & Responsibilities

  • Design and conduct experimental measurements of drone and eVTOL noise in anechoic chambers and outdoor environments.
  • Develop and validate physics-based noise source models for UAV/eVTOL rotors.
  • Process and analyse acoustic measurement data to characterise UAV/eVTOL noise signatures.
  • Build and maintain a comprehensive noise database for environmental impact assessments and regulatory recommendations.
  • Collaborate with team members and publish research findings in high-impact journals and conferences.

Requirements & Qualifications

  • PhD in Aerospace Engineering, Mechanical Engineering, Acoustics, Applied Mathematics, Physics, or related field.
  • Hands-on experience in acoustic or aeroacoustic experiments in anechoic chambers and outdoor environments.
  • Proficiency in acoustic data processing, spectral methods, beamforming, and noise source identification.
  • Strong background in aeroacoustics and rotor/propeller noise mechanisms preferred.
  • Proficiency in programming languages such as MATLAB, Python, Fortran, or C++.
  • Excellent written and oral communication skills in English and ability to work independently and collaboratively.
