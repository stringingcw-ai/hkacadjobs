Duties & Responsibilities

  • Identify, contact and manage relationships with potential founding scholars and institutional partners across Belt and Road countries.
  • Liaise with relevant government departments, international organizations and partner bodies to support the Alliance's activities.
  • Coordinate the preparation and organization of the BR-CESSA Inaugural Summit including scholar invitations, logistics, programme scheduling and on-site support.
  • Organize and administer the Belt and Road International Collaboration Fund, including funding applications, budgeting, financial tracking, procurement and grant reporting.
  • Support the development of joint research proposals and Memoranda of Understanding with partner institutions.
  • Coordinate scientific demonstrations, field visits and technical showcases for partners and visiting delegations.
  • Maintain and expand the Alliance's scholar database and Talent Matching Platform.
  • Manage communications, quarterly webinars, the online collaboration platform, website and documentation.
  • Service the Steering Committee and other committees by preparing meeting materials, minutes and follow-up.
  • Prepare reports and promotional content for internal and external stakeholders.
  • Provide administrative, logistical, data management and financial support to Division faculty members as assigned.

Requirements & Qualifications

  • A doctoral degree is required.
