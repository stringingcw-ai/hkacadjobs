Duties & Responsibilities

  • Contribute to planning, development, implementation and review of the Master of Science in Digital Tourism and Hospitality Management programme.
  • Provide academic leadership in learning, teaching strategies and curriculum development for at least one specialist module.
  • Teach and supervise students at graduate and undergraduate levels in both face-to-face and online formats.
  • Lead scholarly activities including research, consultancy, networking and industry collaborations.
  • Maintain liaison with industry partners and educational institutions to ensure programme relevance and excellence.
  • Oversee the MScDTHM and related programmes and lead accreditation exercises (Professor/Associate Professor rank only).

Requirements & Qualifications

  • Hold a recognised Doctoral degree in Tourism and Hospitality Management or related discipline.
  • Demonstrate relevant teaching experience at tertiary level.
  • Show achievements in research and scholarly activities.
  • Possess professional practice experience in relevant fields.
  • Achieve high proficiency in English and Chinese, including Putonghua.
  • Have mother tongue/language as Putonghua, or meet alternative criteria.
  • Preferably have experience in new programme development and accreditation exercises.
