Duties & Responsibilities

  • Install, commission, operate, and maintain computers, AV, lighting, and related equipment.
  • Assist in setting up IT facilities and services for Institute events.
  • Provide IT technical support to staff and students.
  • Coordinate procurement of consumables and equipment.
  • Maintain store records and assist in inventory control.
  • Generate operational reports and documentation as needed.
  • Assist in network and server administration.
  • Perform other duties as assigned by supervisors.

Requirements & Qualifications

  • Hold a recognised Diploma or Higher Certificate in a relevant discipline.
  • Have at least 2 years of relevant work experience.
  • Be proficient in commonly used computer and educational software such as Microsoft and Adobe.
  • Have good knowledge of AV equipment, computer hardware, and networking operation, maintenance, and troubleshooting.
  • Possess proficiency in written and spoken English and Chinese.
  • Have good communication and interpersonal skills.
  • Be willing to learn the latest IT technologies.
  • Preferably have experience with Windows & Linux servers, Active Directory, network management, and helpdesk systems.

Appointment

  • Full-time; may require work at different sites, irregular hours, shifts, and weekend work as necessary; primarily based at Chai Wan Campus.
