Duties & Responsibilities

  • Assist with acquisition activities including deduplication, quotations, ordering, receiving and inspecting materials.
  • Perform copy cataloguing and administrative duties managing library supplies and equipment.
  • Support learning and teaching enhancement activities and promote library resources.
  • Handle daily operations including counter services, access monitoring, patrols, inventory checks and shelving.
  • Work on shift duties during daytime/evening on weekdays and/or weekends.

Requirements & Qualifications

  • Possess at least Grade E/Level 2 or equivalent in five HKCEE or HKDSEE subjects including English, Chinese and Mathematics.
  • Have five years of relevant working experience.
  • Demonstrate good proficiency in written and spoken English and Chinese.
  • Exhibit strong interpersonal and customer service skills.
  • Be proficient in MS Word, Excel and Chinese word processing.
