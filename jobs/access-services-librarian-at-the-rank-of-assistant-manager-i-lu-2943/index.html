Duties & Responsibilities

  • Plan and implement access services policies, procedures, and resource sharing management.
  • Manage daily library operations including budgets, staff rosters, and statistics reporting.
  • Supervise and train library staff members.
  • Collaborate with the Learning Support team on information literacy and liaison activities.
  • Represent the Library on internal and external committees and task forces.
  • Work shifts during evenings, weekends, and public holidays as required.

Requirements & Qualifications

  • Bachelor's degree in humanities, business, social sciences, or relevant field.
  • Master of Library Science (MLS) or equivalent professional qualification in Librarianship.
  • At least three years of relevant post-qualification experience in libraries, preferably academic libraries.
  • Proven ability to manage public interactions, supervise staff, and organize complex projects.
  • Proficient English and Chinese (Cantonese and Putonghua).
