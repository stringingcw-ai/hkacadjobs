Duties & Responsibilities

  • Lead the School's advanced clinical training and laboratory teaching programmes.
  • Pursue new strategic directions for clinical education development within the School.
  • Contribute to teaching, training, and curriculum development initiatives.

Requirements & Qualifications

  • Hold a Registered Nurse qualification registrable with the Nursing Council of Hong Kong.
  • Possess a doctoral degree in Nursing or a related discipline.
  • Have at least 12 years' clinical experience with local recognition and national or regional evidence of clinical excellence.
  • Demonstrate substantial experience in teaching, training, and/or curriculum development.

Appointment

  • Fixed-term contract — 2 to 3 years with possibility of renewal subject to funding availability and satisfactory performance.
  • Fractional appointment may be considered for suitable candidates.
  • Competitive salary commensurate with qualifications and experience, plus annual leave, medical benefits, contract-end gratuity, retirement scheme contribution (up to 15% of basic salary), and housing benefits as applicable.

Key Dates

  • Start date: As soon as possible.
  • Review of applications start date: September 30, 2026.
  • Application review end date: January 30, 2027 (or until posts are filled, whichever is earlier).
