Duties & Responsibilities

  • Assist the IT Manager in leading the Web Application Team and overseeing daily operations of web systems including admission platforms, websites, and portal services.
  • Manage development, maintenance, enhancement, and integration of web application systems and websites.
  • Lead implementation, upgrades, testing, and deployment of admission systems while maintaining service quality and security standards.
  • Assist with adopting and integrating AI technologies into web applications and work with academic teams to develop AI-driven solutions.
  • Resolve web system issues, manage incidents, and coordinate with internal teams and external vendors for technical improvements.
  • Manage project activities including requirements gathering, solution design, resource planning, and budget monitoring.
  • Advise on and champion IT governance, cybersecurity, data protection, and system administration standards.
  • Supervise and mentor junior staff, promote knowledge sharing, and support succession planning.

Requirements & Qualifications

  • Bachelor's degree in Information Technology, Computer Science, or Software Engineering (Master's degree preferred).
  • Minimum of five years of post-qualification experience in web development, implementation, or management of admission systems.
  • Extensive knowledge of admission systems in higher education institutions and admissions operations.
  • Experience coordinating projects, monitoring tasks, and liaising with vendors and stakeholders.
  • Strong communication, documentation, and stakeholder support capabilities.
  • Practical expertise in front-end frameworks (React, Vue, or Angular) and back-end development, servers, APIs, and databases.
  • Proficiency in SDLC, Agile/Scrum methodologies, Git, and CI/CD pipelines.
  • Knowledge of AI chatbots and responsive design with expertise in data privacy and cybersecurity.
