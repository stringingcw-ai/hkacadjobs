Duties & Responsibilities

  • Provide academic leadership in planning, developing, and reviewing academic programmes in Digital Innovation and Technology.
  • Formulate learning and teaching strategies and develop curriculum modules in Computer Science & Information Technology specializations.
  • Conduct research in Artificial Intelligence, Cyber Security, Human-Computer Interaction, Computer Graphics, Multimedia Technology, or Game Technology.
  • Teach, supervise students, and deliver quality programmes while leading scholarly activities including research, consultancy, and industry collaboration.
  • Maintain relationships with industry, commerce, and educational institutions to ensure programme relevance and excellence.

Requirements & Qualifications

  • Hold a recognised Doctoral degree in Computer Science, Information Science, Artificial Intelligence, Human-Computer Interaction, Computer Graphics, or related field.
  • Demonstrate relevant teaching experience at tertiary level and achievement in research and scholarly activities.
  • Possess professional practice experience in relevant fields.
  • Have strong interpersonal and communication skills with high proficiency in written and spoken English and Chinese, including Putonghua.
  • Industry or professional body linkage experience is advantageous.

Appointment

  • Full-time
  • Position based at Chai Wan Campus
  • Appointee may be required to work at different sites and outside Hong Kong, with irregular hours, shifts, and weekend work as necessary
  • Remuneration package will be competitive and commensurate with qualifications and experience
