Duties & Responsibilities

  • Deliver quality teaching in English-related courses, specifically Linguistics/Applied Linguistics.
  • Assist in curriculum design and development of course materials.
  • Manage programmes and courses.
  • Organise co-curricular activities and support college promotion and admission activities.
  • Perform other academic and administrative duties as required.

Requirements & Qualifications

  • Postgraduate degree (preferably doctoral) in a relevant discipline.
  • At least 2 years of full-time teaching experience at post-secondary level or above.
  • Teaching-related qualifications (e.g. TEFL/TESOL) essential for Academic English.
  • Experience in course leadership, curriculum design, and development of test papers preferred.
  • Experience with AI tools and applications is advantageous.

Appointment

  • Full-time contract — 2 years with possibility of renewal subject to review.
  • Salary commensurate with qualifications and experience.
  • Contract-end gratuity (taxable) payable upon satisfactory completion.
  • Medical and dental benefits, annual leave, and library facilities provided.
  • Staff development opportunities available.
