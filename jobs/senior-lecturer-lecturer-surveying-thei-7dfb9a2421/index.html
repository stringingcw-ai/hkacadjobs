Duties & Responsibilities

  • Report to the Programme Leader and Head of Department.
  • Teach and develop curriculum, learning materials, and assessment methods.
  • Support student learning and supervise student projects.
  • Participate in academic and scholarly activities.
  • Provide academic and administrative support to the Surveying programme.

Requirements & Qualifications

  • Hold a Master's degree, preferably a Doctorate, in Surveying or related discipline.
  • Have at least 3 years of teaching experience at tertiary level and/or relevant industry experience.
  • Demonstrate strong interpersonal and communication skills.
  • Have high proficiency in English; Chinese proficiency is preferred.

Appointment

  • Full-time; rank of Teaching Fellow I/II; based at Chai Wan Campus.
  • May be required to work at different locations, including outside Hong Kong, and work irregular hours, shifts, and weekends.
  • Remuneration is competitive and commensurate with qualifications and experience.
