# Academic Position Summary

Duties & Responsibilities

  • Provide clinical care and education in Implant Dentistry with strong Periodontology knowledge.
  • Develop and maintain a competitive research portfolio in the field.
  • Contribute to undergraduate and postgraduate curricula development.
  • Teach undergraduate Periodontology courses and supervise students.
  • Participate in Faculty and University affairs and knowledge exchange activities.

Requirements & Qualifications

  • Hold a Dental Bachelor's degree or equivalent qualification.
  • Possess clinical specialist qualification or equivalent status in Implant Dentistry, Prosthodontics, or Periodontology.
  • Have advanced training or clinical experience in Implant Dentistry.
  • Hold a Ph.D. degree or equivalent (for Clinical Associate Professor).
  • Demonstrate fluency in spoken and written English.
  • Show evidence of quality research publications and clinical expertise.

Appointment

  • Non-Tenure Track Fractional (50%) Clinical Associate Professor/Clinical Assistant Professor
  • Fixed-term appointment of two to four years with possibility of renewal based on performance
  • Start date: as soon as possible
