Duties & Responsibilities

  • Conduct research on user behaviours, emerging technologies, and industry best practices to inform learning management system development.
  • Design, develop, and maintain responsive websites and web applications using modern frontend and backend technologies.
  • Collaborate with designers and stakeholders to define project requirements and create user-focused solutions.
  • Implement website features, troubleshoot issues, and ensure security and data protection best practices.
  • Stay current with full-stack web development trends and provide technical support to end-users.

Requirements & Qualifications

  • Bachelor's degree or above in Computer Engineering, Computer Science, Web Development, or related discipline.
  • At least 1 year of experience in Full-Stack/Frontend web development with proficiency in Node.js, Express.js, JavaScript, and PostgreSQL.
  • Strong understanding of responsive design principles and cross-browser compatibility.
  • Experience with modern frontend frameworks (e.g., Vue.js) and backend technologies (Node.js, Laravel).
  • Ability to work independently and as part of a team in a fast-paced environment.
  • Preference given to those with LLM integration experience and learning management system knowledge.

Appointment

  • Full-time contract — up to one year on temporary terms, commencing as soon as possible with possibility of renewal subject to satisfactory performance; competitive salary commensurate with qualifications and experience, plus annual leave and medical benefits.
