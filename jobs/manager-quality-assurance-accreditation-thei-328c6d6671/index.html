Manager (Quality Assurance & Accreditation)

Technological and Higher Education Institute of Hong Kong

Department
Quality Assurance and Accreditation Office
Rank
Non-Academic
Position type
Full-time
Reference
QAAO/MGR(QAA)/11/26

Duties & Responsibilities

  • Provide central support and coordinate with internal and external stakeholders on learning programme development, validation, accreditation and institutional reviews.
  • Assist in developing, reviewing and implementing corporate policies on accreditation, quality assurance and enhancement.
  • Support academic departments and administrative units in implementing quality management measures and training.
  • Provide secretarial or membership support to quality assurance-related boards and committees.
  • Assist in managing daily operations and resources of the Quality Assurance and Accreditation Office.

Requirements & Qualifications

  • Bachelor's degree or equivalent, preferably a higher degree, in a relevant discipline or professional qualification.
  • At least 7 years of supervisory/management level experience in accreditation and quality assurance, preferably in self-financing higher education institutions.
  • Solid experience in learning programme design, quality assurance implementation, programme accreditation and institutional review.
  • Proven record of professional development and linkages with regulatory bodies and industries.
  • Understanding of the Hong Kong Qualifications Framework and recent accreditation developments.
  • Experience liaising and negotiating with accreditation authorities, professional bodies and academic personnel.
  • Strong commitment to fostering quality culture and facilitating quality assurance.
  • Good administrative, interpersonal, communication and team-building skills.
  • Ability to work under pressure and meet tight deadlines.
  • High proficiency in English and Chinese, preferably including Putonghua, with strong presentation skills.

Appointment

  • Full-time, rank of Manager; competitive remuneration commensurate with qualifications and experience
