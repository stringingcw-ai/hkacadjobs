**Duties & Responsibilities**

*Lab Assistant:*

  • Conduct quantitative and qualitative research studies including design, data collection, and analysis.
  • Provide administrative support to the laboratory manager and directors.
  • Prepare literature reviews on neuroscience and forensic psychology.
  • Maintain laboratory equipment and ensure safety protocol compliance.
  • Assist with publications and course tutorials.

*Lab Manager:*

  • Oversee daily laboratory operations and ensure safety regulation compliance.
  • Manage budgets, resources, and laboratory inventory.
  • Coordinate research projects including planning, execution, and data analysis.
  • Supervise and mentor lab assistants and interns.
  • Facilitate collaboration among researchers and external partners.

**Requirements & Qualifications**

*Lab Assistant:*

  • Bachelor's degree in Psychology or related field required.
  • Advanced proficiency in SPSS and psychology-related data collection.
  • Fluent in English and Cantonese Chinese.
  • Demonstrate responsibility, industriousness, and willingness to learn.

*Lab Manager:*

  • Master's degree or PhD in Psychology, Neuroscience, or related field required.
  • Experience in laboratory management and research coordination.
  • Advanced SPSS proficiency and knowledge of research methodologies.
  • Strong leadership and mentoring skills.
  • Excellent organizational and communication abilities.

Appointment

  • Full-time, 12–24 months with possibility of renewal subject to mutual agreement
