Duties & Responsibilities

  • Lead digital transformation and smart campus initiatives to enhance operational efficiency and user experience.
  • Ensure teaching spaces, computer labs, and meeting rooms remain fully functional through proactive monitoring and lifecycle management.
  • Oversee IT help desk operations and act as escalation point for complex technical issues.
  • Support development of annual IT operating and capital expenditure budgets by consolidating inputs from multiple departments.
  • Prepare regular financial reports and dashboards for senior management highlighting trends, risks, and cost drivers.
  • Maintain accurate inventory of all IT assets including hardware, software, cloud subscriptions, and licenses.
  • Monitor software license usage and compliance to ensure audit readiness.
  • Track warranty periods, maintenance agreements, support contracts, and subscription expirations.

Requirements & Qualifications

  • 7+ years in IT operations and management services.
  • 2+ years in an IT management or service management role.
  • Proven experience managing IT budgets, vendors, and procurement in medium to large enterprises.
  • Demonstrated ability to manage hardware, software, license, and subscription inventories.
  • Experience in contract review, renewal planning, and commercial negotiation.
