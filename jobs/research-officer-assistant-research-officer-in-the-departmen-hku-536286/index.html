Duties & Responsibilities

  • Design and devise research studies and evaluation frameworks for a large-scale mental health and well-being project in primary schools.
  • Perform administrative tasks supporting the research and intervention project.
  • Supervise junior researchers and research assistants.
  • Manage and coordinate research teams to achieve project objectives.

Requirements & Qualifications

  • Hold a Ph.D. or MPhil degree in Psychology, Social Sciences, or a related discipline.
  • Demonstrate proficiency in advanced statistical software such as R, Stata, or Python.
  • Have competency in MS Excel and Chinese typing.
  • Experience in community or educational research projects is advantageous.

Appointment

  • Full-time, fixed-term contract for two years with possibility of renewal subject to satisfactory performance and funding availability.
  • Contract-end gratuity and University retirement benefits contribution of 10% (Assistant Research Officer) or 15% (Research Officer) of basic salary.
  • Competitive salary commensurate with qualifications and experience, plus annual leave and medical benefits.
  • Rank (Research Officer or Assistant Research Officer) determined by applicant's qualifications and experience.

  • Appointment commencement: September 2026
  • Application review closing date: 12 June 2026 (or when post is filled, whichever is earlier)
