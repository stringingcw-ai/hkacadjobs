Duties & Responsibilities

  • Manage and operate the fitness room, including conducting induction courses for new users.
  • Provide technical support to staff and students using fitness room facilities.
  • Assist in setting up facilities and services for the Department.
  • Set up and support sports representative teams and arrange inter-university sporting events.
  • Assist academic staff in preparing and demonstrating sports-related practical classes and projects.
  • Design training materials and conduct practical training and workshops for students and staff.

Requirements & Qualifications

  • Bachelor's degree or above in Physical Education, Sports, Sports Science, or related discipline.
  • Teaching experience, particularly in higher education or with adults.
  • At least one valid Strength & Conditioning certification (ASCA, UKSCA, or NSCA-CSCS).
  • Proven experience prescribing, delivering, and progressing S&C and personal training programmes.
  • Valid first aid certificate or evidence of working towards one.
  • High proficiency in English and Chinese, preferably including Putonghua.
  • Good communication, interpersonal, and presentation skills.
  • Experience with teenage and adult populations, including high-functioning or professional individuals, is preferred.
