Duties & Responsibilities

  • Oversee daily operations, equipment maintenance, troubleshooting, and ensure minimal downtime.
  • Enforce lab safety, policies, regulatory compliance, and access protocols.
  • Administer fee-for-service schemes, procure and manage chemicals and consumables, and track equipment wear.
  • Prepare administrative, usage, and statistical reports and documents.
  • Manage training schedules, instrument bookings, and user enquiries.
  • Design and deliver instrument training and practical workshops for users.
  • Provide technical consultations on experimental design, sample preparation, and data interpretation.
  • Offer hands-on support and technical assistance during equipment operation.
  • Support research, collaborative, and consultancy projects with technical expertise and facility planning.
  • Organise promotional campaigns, seminars, and engagement events to increase facility utilisation.

Requirements & Qualifications

  • Hold a recognised degree in Chemistry, Biochemistry, Biomedical Sciences, or related discipline.
  • Possess at least three years of relevant experience with solid hands-on instrument operation skills.
  • PhD preferred; Master's or Bachelor's degree with substantial technical experience acceptable.
  • Lab facility management experience in higher education or technical specialist role with instrument manufacturers is advantageous.
  • Demonstrate excellent written and verbal communication in English and Chinese.
  • Show exceptional accuracy, attention to detail, and strong data and safety compliance skills.
  • Display strong interpersonal skills and ability to foster interdisciplinary collaborations.
  • Proficiently manage and analyse operational data using effective presentation tools.
  • Be proactive, reliable, self-motivated, and capable of multitasking in a dynamic environment.
