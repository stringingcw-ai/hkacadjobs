Duties & Responsibilities

  • Perform daily cleaning and sweeping of laboratory spaces and office areas.
  • Wash and maintain laboratory instruments and equipment.
  • Serve as a messenger and handle miscellaneous tasks.
  • Handle infectious substances as part of work duties.
  • Work rotating shifts including alternate Saturdays (half-day).

Requirements & Qualifications

  • Possess Secondary 3 or equivalent education.
  • Demonstrate fluent Cantonese and basic English proficiency.
  • Have 5 or more years of relevant work experience.
  • Preferred: experience in clearing and handling animal remains.
  • Preferred: hold a steam container safety operator certificate.

Appointment

  • Full-time — One-year temporary contract or two-year contract (two-year contract includes end-of-contract gratuity and mandatory provident fund contributions totalling 10% of salary; continuation possible upon satisfactory performance).

Key Dates

  • Recruitment deadline: 2 May 2026 (or until position is filled, whichever is earlier).
