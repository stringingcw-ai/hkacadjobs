Duties & Responsibilities

  • Perform daily accounting operations including journal entries, payment processing, and reconciliations.
  • Prepare monthly reports, variance analyses, and summaries for internal review.
  • Coordinate with banks and counterparties on payment instructions, fund transfers, and settlements.
  • Liaise with internal departments, financial institutions, and external stakeholders to support operations.
  • Assist external auditors and prepare documentation during audit engagements.
  • Participate in process improvement initiatives and assigned projects.

Requirements & Qualifications

  • Bachelor's degree in Accounting, Finance, or a related discipline.
  • 1-3 years of relevant work experience (fresh graduates with strong academics or internship experience welcome).
  • Strong communication abilities and effective stakeholder management skills.
  • Experience with Oracle Financials and Microsoft Office applications is highly desirable.
  • Prior exposure to investment operations, treasury functions, or banking is advantageous but not required.

Appointment

  • Full-time fixed-term contract for 2 years with possibility of renewal, commencing as soon as possible.
