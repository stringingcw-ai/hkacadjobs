Duties & Responsibilities

  • Develop optimization models and algorithms for semiconductor equipment scheduling to reduce cycle times and WIP inventory.
  • Create efficient scheduling algorithms for Automated Material Handling Systems to minimize transport delays and improve material flow.
  • Build a digital twin framework integrating real-time data and simulation for performance monitoring and strategy evaluation.
  • Collaborate with research group members on algorithm development.
  • Deliver research outcomes to industry partners for practical semiconductor manufacturing applications.
  • Write and submit high-quality academic papers.

Requirements & Qualifications

  • Hold a bachelor's or master's degree in Automation, Computer Science, Management Science, Industrial Engineering, or related fields, or be a current PhD student.
  • Be proficient in at least one programming language such as C++ or Python.
  • Have familiarity with scheduling and path planning modeling methods and commercial solvers like CPLEX or GUROBI, with prior academic publishing experience preferred.
  • Demonstrate strong programming skills with preference for those with software development experience.
