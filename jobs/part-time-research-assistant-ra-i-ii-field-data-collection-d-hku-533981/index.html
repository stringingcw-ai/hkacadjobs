Duties & Responsibilities

  • Drive a manual transmission light goods vehicle along pre-designated routes (approximately 100 km per day) across Hong Kong for mobile data collection.
  • Ensure smooth operation of data collection equipment during driving sessions.
  • Maintain accurate records of driving routes, timing, and field notes according to project protocols.
  • Assist with equipment setup, checks, and secure handling before and after each field session.
  • Adhere to all safety, operational, and data quality requirements specified by the research team.

Requirements & Qualifications

  • Hold a valid Hong Kong driving licence (Class 2 - Light Goods Vehicle) and be legally permitted to drive light goods vehicles.
  • Demonstrate ability to drive a manual transmission light goods vehicle.
  • Have familiarity with Hong Kong driving conditions across urban, suburban, and highway environments.
  • Maintain a good driving record over the past three years (subject to verification and driving behaviour testing).
  • Possess strong responsibility, attention to detail, and ability to follow standardized fieldwork protocols.
  • Demonstrate availability for flexible scheduling, including occasional weekend work.
  • Prior research assistance, fieldwork, or data collection experience is advantageous but not required.

Appointment

  • Part-time, hourly basis, January 2026 to June 2026 (project-based with potential for future opportunities).

Key Dates

  • Commencement: January 2026
  • End date: June 2026
  • Application review deadline: June 30, 2026 (or until post is filled, whichever is earlier)
