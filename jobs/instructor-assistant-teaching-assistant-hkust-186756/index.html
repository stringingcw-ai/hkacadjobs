Duties & Responsibilities

  • Support delivery of a new online AI literacy Common Core course for first-year undergraduates across multiple sections.
  • Moderate discussion forums and respond to student queries with timely, accurate feedback.
  • Grade assessments including case study analyses and reflections using consistent rubrics with human oversight.
  • Prepare, update, and quality-assure course materials including videos, quizzes, interactive readings, and digital tasks.
  • Coordinate with Graduate Teaching Assistants and tutors to maintain grading consistency and calibrate assessment standards.
  • Coach student teams and assist with the chatbot competition, including checkpoint reviews and judging.
  • Contribute to course evaluation and continuous improvement based on learning analytics and student feedback.
  • Provide administrative support including record-keeping, scheduling, and communication with departments.

Requirements & Qualifications

  • Bachelor's degree required; master's degree preferred in education, learning sciences, cognitive science, computer science, data science, AI, or related field.
  • Strong interest in AI literacy, human–AI collaboration, and undergraduate teaching.
  • Excellent written and spoken English; working proficiency in Cantonese or Putonghua is advantageous.
  • Strong communication, interpersonal, and organizational skills with ability to work effectively in teams.

Appointment

  • Full-time, fixed-term contract for 1 year, renewable subject to performance and operational needs.
