Duties & Responsibilities

  • Play a key role in strategic planning and academic development of the College.
  • Assist the College Principal in providing academic leadership and overseeing academic divisions.
  • Manage curriculum development, programme delivery, quality assurance and accreditation.
  • Support initiatives to enhance teaching and professional development of academic staff.

Requirements & Qualifications

  • Hold a postgraduate degree, preferably a doctoral degree.
  • Have substantial experience in local tertiary education institutions.
  • Possess considerable knowledge and experience in curriculum development and quality assurance procedures.
  • Demonstrate in-depth understanding of the Hong Kong higher education system and strong networking with local and overseas universities.
  • Be forward-looking with outstanding organisational, influencing, interpersonal and communication skills.
  • Have strong command of written and spoken English and Chinese.
