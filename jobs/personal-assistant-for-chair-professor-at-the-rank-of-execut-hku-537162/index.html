Duties & Responsibilities

  • Manage the Chair Professor's calendar, bookkeeping, and expense management.
  • Handle recruitment, program registration, and event organization with logistics coordination.
  • Maintain and enhance data management systems and website platforms.
  • Facilitate communication and coordination between academic, administrative, and research sub-groups.
  • Work on-site during holidays or outside normal office hours when required.

Requirements & Qualifications

  • Bachelor's degree in Science with at least two years of executive or administrative work experience outside a laboratory setting.
  • Excellent spoken and written English and Chinese (Cantonese and Putonghua).
  • Proactive, solution-oriented with strong organizational and interpersonal skills.
  • Experience in accredited laboratory environments and proven scientific contributions through patents, publications, or protocol design.
  • Essential: Microsoft Office, Adobe Creative Suite, website design, and AI-driven platform proficiency.
  • Advantageous: Master's degree in Chemistry/Management, knowledge of R and Python, management systems experience.

Appointment

  • Full-time, two-year fixed-term contract with contract-end gratuity and University retirement benefits contribution (up to 10% of basic salary), with possibility of renewal subject to funding and performance.
