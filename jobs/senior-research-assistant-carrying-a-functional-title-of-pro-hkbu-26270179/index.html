Duties & Responsibilities

  • Conduct research and development at an off-campus laboratory located at Hong Kong Science and Technology Parks.
  • Manage project administration for research and development initiatives.
  • Participate in research and development of new drug products.
  • Assist with planning, designing and implementing laboratory experiments.
  • Maintain detailed laboratory records and documentation of experimental procedures and results.
  • Support liaison with internal teams and external stakeholders.

Requirements & Qualifications

  • Hold a bachelor's degree or above in science or related field from a recognised university.
  • Possess at least 3 years of post-qualification research experience.
  • Demonstrate hands-on experience with molecular biology and mass spectrometry techniques.
  • Display strong analytical and interpersonal skills with attention to detail.
  • Possess excellent written and verbal communication skills in English, Cantonese, and Putonghua.
