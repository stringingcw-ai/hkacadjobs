Duties & Responsibilities

  • Provide counseling and guidance to local and non-Chinese speaking students on further studies, career development, and fieldwork placement.
  • Market, promote, and implement the People of Different Races Programme for Ethnic Minority Youth.
  • Organize student, office, and extra-curricular activities, plus volunteer services.
  • Carry out student and academic administration duties.

Requirements & Qualifications

  • Hold a recognized degree in Social Work or relevant discipline, preferably with post-graduate qualification.
  • Be registered as a social worker under the Social Workers Registration Ordinance Chapter 505.
  • Have at least 3 years' relevant experience in personal/career counseling, student services administration, and youth work.
  • Demonstrate strong presentation and interpersonal skills with proficiency in English and Chinese.
  • Work independently and under pressure while being a collaborative team player.
  • Understand the learning needs of Non-engaged Youth and have experience with Ethnic Minority Youth.

Appointment

  • Full-time; Term position at the rank of Student Affairs Officer.
