Duties & Responsibilities

  • Administer apprenticeship training schemes according to the Apprenticeship Ordinance.
  • Advise employers on training and employing apprentices.
  • Coordinate complementary training and monitor apprentice study performance.
  • Mentor and coach apprentices throughout their training period.
  • Liaise with internal units and external stakeholders to develop apprenticeship programmes.
  • Assist in complaint investigations and dispute conciliation between employers and apprentices.
  • Lead development of training programmes and work-based assessment activities.
  • Conduct cross-border and overseas visits.
  • Perform quality assurance activities and gather stakeholder feedback.

Requirements & Qualifications

  • Hold a relevant recognised degree or professional qualification, preferably with postgraduate qualification.
  • Have relevant experience in teaching or industry (preferred).
  • Possess mentoring skills and experience in compliance, inspection, or training monitoring (preferred).
  • Have experience in youth training and development (preferred).
  • Be fluent in English and Cantonese with strong communication and presentation skills.
