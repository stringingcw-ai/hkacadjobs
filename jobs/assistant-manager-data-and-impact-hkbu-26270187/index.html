Duties & Responsibilities

  • Analyse institutional, research, and external data to generate clear insights for senior management decision-making.
  • Conduct quantitative and qualitative analyses of institutional performance, research impact, and higher education sector developments.
  • Gather, validate, and synthesise data from institutional systems and external sources to produce accurate and actionable insights.
  • Prepare analytical reports, briefing papers, presentations, and background materials for senior management and stakeholders.
  • Support analyses of the University's performance in global rankings, sustainability assessments, and evaluation frameworks.
  • Undertake research performance and bibliometric analyses and translate findings into practical recommendations.
  • Monitor sector methodologies and indicators, and assess their implications for strategic positioning.
  • Support planning, coordination, and delivery of strategic projects and impact-related initiatives.
  • Contribute to dashboards and decision-support materials that monitor performance against institutional priorities.
  • Work with internal stakeholders to clarify data requirements and ensure data quality.
  • Lead or coordinate assigned workstreams to deliver high-quality outputs within agreed timelines.

Requirements & Qualifications

  • Bachelor's degree in data analytics, statistics, economics, information science, public policy, or related discipline.
  • Postgraduate qualification in a related field is advantageous.
  • Preferably five years of professional experience in data analysis, institutional research, or strategic analysis.
  • Experience in the higher education sector is advantageous.
  • Strong analytical and critical-thinking capabilities with experience interpreting complex data.
  • Sound data-handling skills and high accuracy standards.
  • Excellent written and verbal communication skills.
