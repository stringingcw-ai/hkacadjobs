Duties & Responsibilities

  • Prepare and compile management reports, statistics, proposals, and meeting materials.
  • Organise and provide secretarial support to meetings, committees and boards, including minutes and agendas.
  • Coordinate events, functions and forums for the President's Office or Institute.
  • Prepare official correspondence and liaison with internal and external parties.
  • Provide administrative support to the President's Office operations.
  • Coordinate arrangements for visits by external guests.
  • Handle ad hoc projects and assignments as required.

Requirements & Qualifications

  • A recognised degree or equivalent qualification.
  • At least 2 years' relevant work experience at executive level, preferably in educational institutions.
  • Detail-oriented, organised and proactive with strong time management and multitasking skills.
  • Fluent in written and spoken English and Chinese with strong communication skills.
  • Experience in report writing, meeting minutes and proposal writing is preferred.
  • Able to handle confidential information appropriately.
  • High proficiency in MS Word, MS Excel, MS PowerPoint and Chinese word processing.
