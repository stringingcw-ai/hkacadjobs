Duties & Responsibilities

  • Prepare and compile management reports, statistics, proposals, presentation slides and meeting materials.
  • Organise and provide secretarial support to meetings, committees and boards, including minutes and agendas.
  • Coordinate and support various events, functions and forums for the President's Office or Institute.
  • Prepare official correspondence and liaise with internal and external parties.
  • Provide administrative support to the President's Office operations.
  • Coordinate arrangements for visits by external guests.
  • Handle ad hoc projects and assignments as required.

Requirements & Qualifications

  • Hold a recognised degree or equivalent qualification.
  • Have at least 2 years' relevant work experience at executive level, preferably in educational institutions.
  • Be detail-oriented, organised, proactive and able to multi-task independently and work as a team player.
  • Have strong command of written and spoken English and Chinese with excellent communication skills.
  • Preferably have solid experience in report writing, meeting minutes and proposal writing.
  • Be able to handle confidential information appropriately.
  • Have high proficiency in MS Word, MS Excel, MS PowerPoint and Chinese word processing.

Appointment

  • Full-time; Work at Chai Wan Campus; May be required to perform duties at different sites and outside Hong Kong, and work irregular hours, shifts and weekends as necessary.
