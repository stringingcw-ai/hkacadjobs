Senior Finance Manager (External Financing and Treasury)

Duties & Responsibilities

  • Lead external financing projects including RFP tender document preparation for bond issuance, banking facilities, and alternative financing options.
  • Evaluate RFP proposals and present detailed analysis findings to management.
  • Prepare committee papers and presentations with recommendations for Finance Committee and Standing Committee approval.
  • Serve as recording secretary for the Task Force on External Financing Exercises.
  • Supervise preparation of weekly or regular cashflow and financing forecasts.
  • Assist in reporting on accounting operations, financial management, budgeting, and auditing of external financing arrangements.
  • Monitor financial operations and performance of PRC subsidiaries and joint ventures for regulatory compliance.
  • Conduct periodic reviews of accounting policies and procedures.

Requirements & Qualifications

  • Bachelor's degree in Accounting with minimum eight years of relevant experience, including five years supervisory experience.
  • Qualified accountant with membership of a recognised professional accounting body.
  • Chartered Financial Analyst (CFA) qualification preferred.
  • Minimum three years' prior experience in external financing and corporate investment portfolio management.
  • Strong analytical and problem-solving skills with excellent interpersonal abilities.
  • Detailed-minded with strong numerical sense and planning/execution capabilities.
  • Strong leadership and organisational skills in coaching and talent management.
  • Excellent presentation and communication skills for conveying complex financial information.
  • Ability to work under pressure independently and meet tight deadlines.
  • Good command of written and spoken English.
