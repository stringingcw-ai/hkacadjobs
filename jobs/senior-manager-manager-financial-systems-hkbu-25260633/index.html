Duties & Responsibilities

  • Lead financial systems implementation including project coordination, testing, and user training.
  • Manage external vendors and consultants to ensure quality deliverables meet timelines.
  • Identify process improvements, system enhancements, and automation opportunities to optimize operations.
  • Design and implement automated reporting solutions and dashboards aligned with business objectives.
  • Oversee data pipeline design and maintenance integrating data from multiple sources.
  • Develop interactive dashboards providing real-time insights for data-driven decision-making.
  • Create and maintain comprehensive data catalogues with effective documentation and controls.
  • Provide advisory comments on system-related projects.

Requirements & Qualifications

  • Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Information Systems, Accounting, or related discipline.
  • 8+ years of relevant work experience (or less for Manager-level consideration).
  • Expertise in ETL technologies such as Alteryx and Python.
  • Proficiency in data visualization tools like Power BI and Qlik Sense.
  • Knowledge of ERP systems (Oracle EBS) and Hyperion is advantageous.
  • Strong computer literacy in Microsoft Windows and Microsoft 365.
  • Strong analytical and interpersonal skills with effective communication abilities.
  • Fluent in written and spoken English and Chinese.

Appointment

  • Full-time fixed-term contract of three years with re-appointment subject to mutual agreement and funding availability.

Key Dates

  • Review date: 8 weeks after application submission (unsuccessful if not invited for interview by this date).
