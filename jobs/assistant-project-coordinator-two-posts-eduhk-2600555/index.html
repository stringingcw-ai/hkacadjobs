Duties & Responsibilities

  • Coordinate project implementation and service delivery for ASD student transition programs.
  • Liaise with students, parents, schools, employers, and community partners.
  • Organize and run job-related social skills training groups and career development activities.
  • Assist in participant recruitment, assessment, and data collection.
  • Support development, testing, and maintenance of web-based career planning tools and online resources.
  • Prepare project materials, records, reports, and research outputs.
  • Organize workshops, interviews, meetings, and dissemination activities.
  • Assist in evaluating project outcomes.

Requirements & Qualifications

  • Bachelor's degree or above in psychology, education, social work, social sciences, information technology, or related discipline.
  • Strong IT skills and proficiency in Microsoft Office suite.
  • Strong organizational, communication, and interpersonal skills.
  • Experience in project coordination, research support, or community-based intervention projects is preferred.
  • Experience with ASD individuals, special educational needs, or schools is advantageous.
  • Experience in participant recruitment, data collection, report writing, and event coordination is highly desirable.
  • Familiarity with online platforms and digital resource development is advantageous.

Appointment

  • Full-time, contract position for 24 months with possibility of renewal subject to mutual agreement.
