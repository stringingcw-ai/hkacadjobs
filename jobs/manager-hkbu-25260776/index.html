Duties & Responsibilities

  • Lead administration of health, life sciences, and innovation & technology funding schemes and related programmes.
  • Provide leadership on research ethics governance and drive process improvements across the grant lifecycle.
  • Supervise and develop team members while setting priorities and ensuring compliance with funder requirements.
  • Develop strategies to promote research, build stakeholder networks, and maintain grant data and statistical reports.

Requirements & Qualifications

  • Minimum 7 years of relevant work experience, preferably in higher education.
  • Experience with RGC grants and statistical report preparation is advantageous.
  • Excellent written and spoken English and Chinese, including Putonghua.
  • Strong project management skills, self-motivation, and ability to work independently in dynamic environments.
  • Meticulous attention to detail, multitasking ability, strong team spirit, and capacity to handle complexity.

Appointment

  • Fixed-term contract — 2 years (re-appointment subject to mutual agreement and funding availability).

  • Review date: 8 weeks after application submission (unsuccessful applicants notified if not invited for interview).
