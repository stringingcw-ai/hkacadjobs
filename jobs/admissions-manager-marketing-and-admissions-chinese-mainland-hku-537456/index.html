Duties & Responsibilities

  • Support the Assistant Director in leading admissions and partnership development for the Chinese Mainland market.
  • Lead recruitment and partnership-building initiatives, including information sessions, sample classes, and networking events.
  • Plan and oversee flagship outreach initiatives such as summer camps, partner conferences, and recruitment activities.
  • Manage end-to-end admissions, recruitment, and partnership development initiatives independently.
  • Develop and maintain relationships with universities, faculties, and partner organisations.
  • Conduct admissions data analysis, reporting, and prepare presentations and management reports.
  • Draft and manage Memoranda of Understanding (MoUs) with partner organisations.

Requirements & Qualifications

  • Hold a bachelor's degree, preferably with a master's qualification.
  • Possess at least five years of full-time experience in business development, marketing, admissions, event management, or client-facing roles.
  • Demonstrate proven expertise and in-depth knowledge of the Chinese Mainland market.
  • Have a successful track record of engaging and negotiating with local stakeholders.
  • Possess strong interpersonal, communication, organisational, and project management skills.
  • Demonstrate excellent attention to detail and sound judgment.
  • Have strong analytical and problem-solving skills with proficiency in Microsoft Excel and PowerPoint.
  • Have excellent command of written and spoken English and Chinese (Putonghua required; Cantonese an advantage).
  • Experience in higher education is advantageous but not required.

Appointment

  • Full-time, two-year fixed-term contract with contract-end gratuity and University contribution to retirement benefits scheme (up to 10% of basic salary); possibility of renewal subject to funding availability and satisfactory performance.
