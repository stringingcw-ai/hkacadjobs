Duties & Responsibilities

  • Conduct literature reviews and develop theoretical frameworks for mental health research.
  • Perform mixed-methods research including experiments, longitudinal studies, and qualitative interviews under PI supervision.
  • Conduct quantitative and qualitative data analysis using statistical and specialized software.
  • Collaborate with community partners on knowledge translation initiatives.
  • Assist with project management, ethics applications, data collection, and preparation of reports and publications.

Requirements & Qualifications

  • Senior Research Assistant: Master's degree in Social Sciences with at least 1 year post-qualification research experience.
  • Research Assistant I/II: Master's degree in Social Sciences or Bachelor's degree with substantial research experience.
  • Proficiency in statistical software (STATA, SPSS, R, or Python) and qualitative analysis software (NVivo).
  • Strong analytical, communication, and interpersonal skills with ability to work independently and collaboratively.
  • Fluency in written and spoken English; Cantonese proficiency advantageous for fieldwork.

Appointment

  • Full-time, one-year temporary contract with possibility of renewal subject to satisfactory performance and funding availability; University contribution to retirement benefits scheme.
