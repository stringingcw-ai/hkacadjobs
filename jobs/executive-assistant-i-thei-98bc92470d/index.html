Duties & Responsibilities

  • Provide secretarial and administrative support to the Registry.
  • Support processing of student visa applications, records management, tuition fees, admissions, examinations, and graduation ceremonies.
  • Supervise junior staff and monitor their performance.
  • Liaise with internal and external parties regarding student-related enquiries.
  • Draft official correspondence including emails, letters, and notices.
  • Handle office administration, supply ordering, stock management, and asset management.
  • Assist with document management and compile management reports and statistics.
  • Provide logistical support for Institute events and activities.
  • Maintain the Registry website.

Requirements & Qualifications

  • Hold Level 2 or above in 5 subjects including English, Chinese, and Mathematics in HKDSE, or equivalent qualifications.
  • Have at least 3 years' relevant work experience as an Executive Assistant or equivalent rank.
  • Demonstrate high proficiency in MS Word, Excel, PowerPoint, and Chinese word processing.
  • Be flexible, detail-oriented, organised, proactive, and able to multitask independently.
  • Handle confidential information appropriately.
  • Possess strong communication and interpersonal skills.
  • Have good command of written and spoken English and Chinese, preferably including Putonghua.
