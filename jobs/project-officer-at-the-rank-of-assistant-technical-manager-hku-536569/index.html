Duties & Responsibilities

  • Facilitate coordination and promotion of innovation projects.

  • Ability to work independently with initiative and under pressure.
  • Excellent organizational and time-management skills to handle multiple assignments and tight deadlines.
  • Strong communication and interpersonal skills to engage diverse stakeholders.
  • Excellent command of spoken and written English and Chinese (Cantonese and Putonghua).
