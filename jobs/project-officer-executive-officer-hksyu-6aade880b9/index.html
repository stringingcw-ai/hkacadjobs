Duties & Responsibilities

  • Gather and maintain proper documentation and records.
  • Support evaluation and research studies.
  • Liaise with internal units, external suppliers, and stakeholders.
  • Coordinate with co-organizers and partner schools to facilitate project implementation.
  • Organize events, including public education activities and training sessions.

  • Support enhancement of gamified e-learning platform, mobile application, and educational materials.
  • Supervise and provide guidance to student helpers.

  • Bachelor's degree in Social Sciences, Marketing, Business Administration, or related discipline.
  • Preferably 2–3 years of relevant working experience; tertiary education sector experience is advantageous.
  • Good command of written and spoken English and Chinese (including Putonghua).
  • Proficiency in common office applications (MS Office Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Outlook).
  • Ability to work both independently and collaboratively with efficiency and accuracy.
  • Strong organizational skill and attention to detail.
