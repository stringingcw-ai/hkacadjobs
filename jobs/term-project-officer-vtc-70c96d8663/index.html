Duties & Responsibilities

  • Supervise planning, management and execution of fashion events with internal and external stakeholders.
  • Solicit sponsorship for projects, functions and events.
  • Support coordination of local, Mainland and international B2B fashion events and visits.
  • Assist with financial and supply matters including procurement, budgeting and sponsorship arrangements.
  • Coordinate and execute promotional or marketing activities and events.
  • Conduct event evaluation and analysis.
  • Prepare proposals and reports for events.
  • Facilitate overseas delegation trips connecting tradeshow organisers, designers and potential buyers.
  • Liaise with the Cultural and Creative Industries Development Agency on communication, PR and budget matters.

Requirements & Qualifications

  • A recognised degree in Design or related discipline, preferably with a post-graduate qualification.
  • At least 5 years of relevant design-related industrial experience.
  • Preferably experience in fashion design, event planning, project management and government funding applications.
  • Good presentation, administrative, interpersonal and team-building skills.
  • High proficiency in written and spoken Chinese and English, preferably including Putonghua.
  • Proficiency in Adobe Creative Suite (Photoshop, Illustrator, InDesign).
  • High motivation for course or new project development.
  • Industry and professional body linkages are an advantage.
