Duties & Responsibilities

  • Oversee daily operations of the Library Systems and Technology Section.
  • Plan, coordinate, and manage library systems and technology activities.
  • Formulate policies and procedures related to library systems and technology.
  • Administer the Alma integrated library system, link resolver, Primo discovery portal, and other digital systems.
  • Develop and maintain the Library's application programmes and database systems.
  • Perform other duties as assigned by the University Librarian.

Requirements & Qualifications

  • At least 2 years' relevant post-qualification experience for Assistant Manager level.
  • Substantial relevant post-qualification experience in academic library systems is advantageous.
  • Hands-on experience in managing software, hardware, and networking across various operating systems is advantageous.
  • Working knowledge of HTML, XML, Java, JavaScript, CSS, PHP, MySQL is preferred.
  • Good interpersonal and communication skills in English and Chinese.
  • Less experienced candidates may be considered for Library Officer level.
