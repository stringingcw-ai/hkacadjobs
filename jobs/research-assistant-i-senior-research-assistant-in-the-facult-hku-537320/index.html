Duties & Responsibilities

  • Analyze approximately 2,300+ drug trafficking sentencing judgments from Hong Kong courts using quantitative and socio-legal methods.
  • Conduct descriptive and inferential statistical analysis including regression, group comparisons, and trend analysis.
  • Lead the writing of an in-depth report on drug trafficking trends and sentencing patterns for an NGO partner.
  • Examine defendant characteristics, trafficking behaviors, sentencing factors, and outcomes from court judgments.
  • Draft a comprehensive, practitioner-oriented report relating findings to youth crime prevention and intervention strategies.

Requirements & Qualifications

  • Master's degree or above in socio-legal studies, Social Work, Criminology, Statistics/Data Science, Public Policy, or related field.
  • Training in descriptive statistics, inferential analysis, and data visualization.
  • Competence in at least one data analysis tool (R, Python, Stata, SPSS, or similar).
  • Demonstrated ability to write clear, structured analytical reports in English.
  • Ability to interpret complex legal judgments and social data independently.
  • Law degree or legal training is advantageous but not a substitute for quantitative methods skills.

Appointment

  • Full-time, three-month temporary contract
  • Senior Research Assistant appointment may be offered to candidates with a Ph.D. or equivalent research experience.
