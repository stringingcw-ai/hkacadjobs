Duties & Responsibilities

  • Conduct independent and collaborative scholarly research and publish in high-impact journals.
  • Attract competitive grants to maintain active research programmes.
  • Teach undergraduate (especially MBBS) and postgraduate programmes and supervise research students.
  • Contribute to professional education programmes and community public health initiatives.
  • Perform administrative duties as required.

Requirements & Qualifications

  • Possess a primary medical qualification registrable in Hong Kong.
  • Hold a specialist qualification in Public Health Medicine (F.H.K.A.M. Community Medicine) or equivalent.
  • Demonstrate proven ability to conduct quality research and publish in high-quality journals.
  • Show track record or strong commitment to teaching at undergraduate and postgraduate levels.
  • Evidence of teaching and leadership in professional education or community engagement.

Appointment

  • Full-time, four-year fixed-term contract with possibility of renewal and consideration for tenure before expiry of second four-year contract; exceptional candidates may be appointed on tenure terms.
