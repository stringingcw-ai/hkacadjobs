Technical Instructor

Hong Kong Baptist University

Department
School of Chinese Medicine
Rank
Senior Lecturer/Lecturer
Position type
Full-time
Reference

Duties & Responsibilities

  • Provide teaching and learning support to taught postgraduate and sub-degree programmes.
  • Manage administrative tasks including Moodle course sites, student recruitment, admissions, and timetabling.
  • Coordinate practice-based learning such as clinical attachments and field visits.
  • Provide technical support for laboratory and practicum teaching, including equipment maintenance and safety compliance.
  • Support quality assurance, accreditation documentation, and data reporting.

Requirements & Qualifications

  • Preferably possess a master's degree in Chinese Medicine or Pharmacy in Chinese Medicine.
  • Demonstrate proficiency in Microsoft Office (Excel, Word, PowerPoint).
  • Have strong written and spoken English and Chinese (Cantonese and Putonghua).
  • Possess excellent communication and interpersonal skills.
  • Teaching experience in higher education, Chinese Medicine Practitioners Licensing Examination pass, or Licensed Pharmacist status is advantageous.

Appointment

  • Fixed-term contract of one to two years; re-appointment subject to mutual agreement and funding availability.

Key Dates

  • Review date: 8 weeks after submission of application (unsuccessful candidates will be notified).
  • Application review: Ongoing until position is filled.
