Duties & Responsibilities

  • Plan and organize alumni networking and volunteering events with strong project management and budget oversight skills.
  • Provide administrative and secretarial support to the Alumni Council and its committees.
  • Oversee alumni membership registration, conduct membership drives, and negotiate external company benefits for alumni.
  • Manage and develop content for alumni communication channels including eNews and website maintenance.
  • Support fundraising strategies, organize fundraising events, solicit donors, and implement donor recognition programmes.
  • Assist subject-specific alumni groups in establishing their own associations.
  • Propose innovative ideas and continuously improve alumni engagement methods.
  • Support implementation of the new alumni database system and ensure project completion.
  • Supervise support staff and ensure efficient office operations.

Requirements & Qualifications

  • Bachelor's degree with at least 4 years of relevant experience, preferably in alumni affairs, communications, and marketing.
  • Experience in committee service and providing secretariat support is preferred.
  • Excellent command of written and spoken English and Chinese (including Putonghua) required.
  • Outgoing, independent, self-motivated, meticulous, and committed personality.
  • Outstanding interpersonal skills and good understanding of people dynamics.
  • Ability to work under pressure and outside office hours.
  • Experience with AI tools and applications is an advantage.

Appointment

  • Contract — 2 years with possibility of renewal subject to review; contract-end gratuity payable upon satisfactory completion; medical and dental benefits, 18 days' annual leave, and library facilities provided; staff development opportunities available.
