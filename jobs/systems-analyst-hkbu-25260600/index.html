Systems Analyst

Duties & Responsibilities

  • Map and document interfaces between legacy and new systems to ensure seamless integration and efficient data transfer.
  • Perform software development lifecycle tasks including requirements gathering, design, development, testing, implementation, and maintenance.
  • Develop innovative solutions and undertake product development.
  • Create and maintain comprehensive testing documentation for complete systems with rigorous quality control.
  • Produce user manuals for new systems to facilitate smooth adoption and maximize system utilization.

Requirements & Qualifications

  • Bachelor's degree in computer science, information technology, or related discipline.
  • At least 3-4 years of relevant work experience with focus on web application development and system integration.
  • Strong proven experience in Java, PHP, Python, RESTful API, and SQL.
  • Knowledge of Docker, Kubernetes, MS Power Platform, PowerBuilder, Jira, or test automation tools is advantageous.
  • Excellent analytical and problem-solving skills with ability to address complex system issues.
  • Solid written and verbal communication skills for technical and non-technical audiences.

Appointment

  • Full-time; fixed-term contract of one year with re-appointment subject to mutual agreement and funding availability.

Key Dates

  • Review date: 8 weeks after application submission (unsuccessful if not invited for interview by this date).
