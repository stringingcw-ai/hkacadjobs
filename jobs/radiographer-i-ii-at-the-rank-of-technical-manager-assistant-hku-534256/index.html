Duties & Responsibilities

  • Assist in performing PET-CT and CT scans and MRIs for diagnosing illnesses and conditions.
  • Receive on-the-job training as necessary.
  • Pursue continuing professional development opportunities.

Requirements & Qualifications

  • Bachelor's degree in Diagnostic Radiography or equivalent qualification.
  • Registration as a Radiographer with the Hong Kong Radiographers Board.
  • At least one year of relevant experience.
  • Proficiency in spoken and written English and Chinese (including Cantonese).
  • Excellent interpersonal, communication, and patient handling skills with empathy.
  • Experience in MRI, NM, CT, or PET/CT is advantageous.

Appointment

  • Fixed-term contract for two years with possibility of renewal, commencing as soon as possible; includes contract-end gratuity and up to 10% University contribution to retirement benefits scheme; competitive salary commensurate with qualifications and experience; annual leave and medical benefits provided.
