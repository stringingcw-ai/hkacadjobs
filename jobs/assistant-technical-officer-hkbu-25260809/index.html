Assistant Technical Officer

Duties & Responsibilities

  • Provide support to studio art classes and assist teaching staff with technical demonstrations.
  • Manage day-to-day operations, material procurement, and equipment maintenance in studios and workshops.
  • Assist students with lab/studio equipment usage and provide technical guidance.
  • Support administrative duties across various workshops and studios at the campus.

Requirements & Qualifications

  • Bachelor's degree preferably in visual arts or related disciplines.
  • Strong experience and knowledge in studio/workshop operations and equipment across sculpture, printmaking, 3D printing, metalworking, and similar areas.
  • Proficiency in software such as Grasshopper, Blender, Fusion360, Rhino, or Cura is advantageous.
  • Fluent written and spoken English, Cantonese, and Putonghua.
  • Strong interpersonal, communication, and organizational skills with ability to work in teams.
  • Proficiency in MS Office applications including Word, Excel, and PowerPoint.

Appointment

  • Fixed-term contract up to two years, with re-appointment subject to mutual agreement and funding availability.
