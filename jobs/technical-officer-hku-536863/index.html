Duties & Responsibilities

  • Manage and maintain the Department's IT infrastructure and computing facilities.
  • Provide technical support and perform hardware/software troubleshooting.
  • Assist in laboratory development and research activities.
  • Develop and deliver capstone and experiential learning courses for the Bachelor of Engineering programme.
  • Coordinate laboratory maintenance work and set up new laboratory facilities.

Requirements & Qualifications

  • Bachelor's degree or equivalent in Computer Science, IT, Computer Engineering, or related disciplines.
  • Relevant post-degree work experience and/or postgraduate research experience.
  • Proficiency in written and spoken English and Chinese.
  • Strong hands-on skills in network administration, server maintenance, and troubleshooting.
  • Foundational understanding of cybersecurity.
  • Good interpersonal and communication skills, self-motivated, and organized.
  • Experience with embedded systems, digital twins, or cyber-physical systems is desirable.
  • Experience with 3D printing, robotics, ERP/WMS systems, IoT, or mobile/web applications is advantageous.

Appointment

  • Full-time, two-year fixed-term contract with possibility of renewal; includes contract-end gratuity and up to 10% of basic salary as University retirement benefit contribution.

Key Dates

  • Application review commencement: As soon as possible.
  • Application deadline: September 30, 2026 (or until post is filled, whichever is earlier).
