Duties & Responsibilities

  • Assist the Head in developing, implementing, and reviewing strategic and operational plans for Centre development.
  • Manage financial planning, accounts, and contribute budget recommendations.
  • Provide secretarial and administrative support to the Programme Management Committee.
  • Oversee quality of administrative and logistical support from office and technical staff.
  • Manage implementation of self-funded programmes and courses.
  • Handle full staff lifecycle human resources matters including recruitment and deployment.
  • Mentor General Office team members for professional development.
  • Assess impact of administrative decisions on stakeholders and improve office processes.

Requirements & Qualifications

  • Bachelor's degree or higher with at least seven years of post-qualification work experience.
  • Excellent written and oral proficiency in English, Chinese, and Putonghua.
  • Strong communication and interpersonal skills.
  • Solid analytical and problem-solving skills with ability to work independently.
  • Well-organised with strong digital literacy and comfort with cloud-based collaboration tools.
  • Self-motivated and highly responsible.
