Duties & Responsibilities

  • Deliver teaching and learning activities to support students' academic language development.
  • Develop and revise programme curriculum to meet academic learning standards.
  • Develop learning and teaching materials using AI tools and new technologies.
  • Monitor and assess student progress and provide constructive feedback.
  • Collaborate with the Centre for Language Enhancement to foster a dynamic learning environment.
  • Participate in academic and scholarly activities contributing to research and professional development.
  • Provide academic and administrative support services to the School and Institute.

Requirements & Qualifications

  • Master's degree (preferably Doctoral degree) in TESOL, English Language, Linguistics, or related disciplines.
  • At least 3 years of experience teaching academic English at tertiary level.
  • Experience in research project funding applications and language centre coordination.
  • Native or native-like English proficiency.
  • Good communication, interpersonal, and presentation skills.
  • Good command of Putonghua and/or Cantonese preferred.
  • Strong team player with ability to work independently and embrace challenges.

Appointment

  • Full-time; short-term contract basis possible; rank of Teaching Fellow I/II.
