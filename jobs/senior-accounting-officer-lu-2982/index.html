Duties & Responsibilities

  • Compile and consolidate user requirements for implementing a new accounting system within the Enterprise Resources Planning System.
  • Review existing workflows and develop new workflows to enhance efficiency with the new system.
  • Suggest system configurations to meet the University's business needs.
  • Identify functional gaps and collaborate with IT and contractors on necessary programming or integration work.
  • Conduct user acceptance testing for various software configurations and newly developed modules.

Requirements & Qualifications

  • Hold a recognized degree or equivalent qualifications in Accounting or related disciplines.
  • Be a member of HKICPA, ACCA, or other relevant professional bodies.
  • Have a minimum of 5 years of supervisory experience, including 2-3 years in integrated accounting system implementation.
  • Demonstrate strong organizational, problem-solving, and presentation skills.
  • Have excellent PC skills in MS Office and good knowledge of computerized accounting systems.
