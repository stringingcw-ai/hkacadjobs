Duties & Responsibilities

  • Manage acquisition activities including deduplication, quotations, ordering, and receiving materials.
  • Perform copy cataloguing and liaise with library vendors and binders.
  • Assist in planning learning and teaching enhancement activities and promoting library resources.
  • Provide administrative support for technical processing of library materials.
  • Maintain library systems and databases, and assist in website and web application development.
  • Prepare statistical reports and perform user services tasks including circulation and shelving.
  • Handle user enquiries and assist in supervision of user areas and promotion of library activities.

Requirements & Qualifications

  • At least Grade E/Level 2 in five HKCEE/HKDSEE subjects including English, Chinese, and Mathematics.
  • Five years of relevant working experience.
  • Good proficiency in written and spoken English and Chinese.
  • Good interpersonal and customer service skills.
  • Proficiency in MS Word, Excel, and Chinese word processing.

Appointment

  • Full-time, 2-year contract (renewable)
