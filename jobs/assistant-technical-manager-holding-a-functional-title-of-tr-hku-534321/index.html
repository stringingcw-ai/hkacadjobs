Duties & Responsibilities

  • Coordinate all training and capacity building activities for the institute.
  • Establish and support a Master of Public Health program in French at a partner university.
  • Develop and deliver targeted short-term trainings in biostatistics, epidemiology, genetics, laboratory skills, and grant writing.
  • Assess training needs, develop content, coordinate logistics, and evaluate program effectiveness.
  • Manage scheduling, attendance, training records, and capacity-building trainings for organizational functions.

Requirements & Qualifications

  • Bachelor's degree in Education or Public Health-related field required; Master's degree is advantageous.
  • 2–5 years of experience in training, academic program development, or related role.
  • Native or near-native proficiency in French with excellent written and spoken skills.
  • Good command of written and spoken English.
  • Familiarity with basic public health concepts, curriculum structures, and experience working with subject matter experts.
  • International organization experience, low- and middle-income country background, and cross-cultural competence are assets.
  • Willingness to undertake frequent international travel.

Appointment

  • Full-time; one-year temporary or two-year fixed-term contract with possible renewal subject to funding and performance.
