Duties & Responsibilities

  • Teach courses for the MA in Intercultural Studies and MA in Cultural Management programmes at multiple degree levels.
  • Conduct innovative research in non-Western environmental media, with focus on inter-Asian or South-South perspectives.
  • Contribute to departmental research initiatives and scholarly community engagement.

Requirements & Qualifications

  • PhD in Film and Media Studies, Literary and Cultural Studies, Arts and Humanities, or a related field.
  • Expertise in non-Western environmental media, cultural practices, and familiarity with Asian and Global South environmental issues.
  • Strong research interests in areas such as environmental humanities, ecocinema, critical ecologies, blue humanities, or media infrastructure.

Appointment

  • Full-time, 3-year contract with possibility of longer-term appointment or tenure.
