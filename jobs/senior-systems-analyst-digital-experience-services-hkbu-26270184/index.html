Duties & Responsibilities

  • Resolve complex technical issues across digital experience platforms and enterprise systems.
  • Provide full-stack development and troubleshooting support for front-end, back-end, integration, and infrastructure components.
  • Support and review development on CMS platforms, particularly Adobe Experience Manager and other content management systems.
  • Develop technical guidelines, standards, and administrator documentation for internal use.
  • Prepare and validate proof-of-concept solutions and optimise system performance through analysis and strategic implementation.
  • Apply performance solutions such as caching strategies, CDN usage, and Apache rewrite rules.
  • Collaborate with IT teams to address security risks, integration issues, and environment problems.
  • Support security hardening, vulnerability fixing, and compliance activities.
  • Leverage analytics and performance metrics to improve digital experience and user engagement.

Requirements & Qualifications

  • Bachelor's degree in computer engineering, computer science, or related discipline.
  • At least 6 years of relevant experience in system development and system analysis roles.
  • Strong full-stack development experience, including Java and object-oriented programming.
  • Solid hands-on experience with CMS platforms, particularly Adobe Experience Manager.
  • Experience in web and platform performance optimisation, including network latency reduction.
  • Practical experience with Apache rewrite rules, caching concepts, CDN, and DNS configuration.
  • Experience addressing application and platform security concerns and vulnerability remediation.
