Duties & Responsibilities

  • Provide academic leadership in planning, designing, and developing training programmes, curricula, and assessment materials.
  • Lead day-to-day operations of the Aviation and Maritime Education Centre, including training delivery and resource management.
  • Lead strategic development of training facilities and equipment aligned with industry needs.
  • Build and maintain relationships with aviation industry partners, professional bodies, and government departments.
  • Develop and review operational policies ensuring regulatory compliance, quality, and safety standards.
  • Contribute to strategic development by exploring training trends and supporting continuous improvement programmes.

Requirements & Qualifications

  • Hold a recognised degree in Mechanical Engineering, Electrical and Electronic Engineering, Aviation Engineering, or equivalent.
  • Possess at least 5 years' relevant industry or training experience in aviation operations or aircraft maintenance.
  • Demonstrate strong networking and liaison skills with industry partners and professional bodies.
  • Have knowledge and experience in procurement procedures, tender administration, and contract management (preferred).
  • Exhibit excellent administrative, interpersonal, communication, and team-building skills.
  • Demonstrate strong presentation skills and high proficiency in English and Chinese, including Putonghua.

  • Full-time contract until 31 August 2028.
