Duties & Responsibilities

  • Conduct independent and collaborative scholarly research in global health.
  • Publish research articles in high-impact journals and attract competitive grants.
  • Teach undergraduate and postgraduate programmes and supervise research students.
  • Perform administrative duties within the School of Public Health.
  • Lead and document field interventions and engage with global health partners.

Requirements & Qualifications

  • Ph.D. degree or equivalent in public health, epidemiology, economics, political science, geography, medical anthropology, medical sociology, international health law, or related discipline.
  • Prior research experience in development studies, health systems research, global public health, primary health care, or implementation science.
  • Strong track record of high-quality research demonstrated by publications and external grants.
  • International recognition with ability to work across disciplines on new research initiatives.
  • Commitment to teaching at undergraduate and postgraduate levels.

Appointment

  • Tenure-track position with three-year fixed-term contract and possibility of renewal.
  • Consideration for tenure before expiry of second three-year fixed-term contract.
  • Exceptionally outstanding candidates may be considered for appointment on tenure terms.
  • Appointment rank determined based on qualifications and experience.
