Duties & Responsibilities

  • Perform phlebotomy procedures on study subjects with precision and care.
  • Assist in participant recruitment and follow-up activities at community elderly centers.
  • Label, process, and store biological specimens according to study protocols.
  • Work at Hong Kong Science Park and travel to various community locations for data collection.
  • Accommodate flexible work schedules including early mornings and weekends as needed.

Requirements & Qualifications

  • Hold at least HKCEE 5 passes (including English, Chinese, Mathematics) or equivalent HKDSE qualifications.
  • Be a registered phlebotomist or hold a Fundamental Venipuncture Techniques certificate.
  • Have at least two years' working experience in phlebotomy.
  • Possess good written and spoken English and Cantonese with proficient computer literacy.
  • Demonstrate strong teamwork, meticulousness, and interpersonal communication skills.

Appointment

  • Full-time, 1 year contract (renewable)
