Duties & Responsibilities

  • Plan, implement, and monitor project activities to ensure timely execution aligned with milestones.
  • Design and conduct evaluation studies including survey design, data collection, analysis, and reporting.
  • Prepare progress reports, presentations, and evaluation summaries for stakeholders.
  • Organize and facilitate workshops, seminars, meetings, and training events.
  • Support student-led activities and oversee the Student Ambassador Program.
  • Develop promotional materials including posters, infographics, and social media content.
  • Update and maintain the project website and online learning platform.
  • Serve as key contact for partner institutions, collaborators, and technical vendors.
  • Maintain project documentation, track budgets, and prepare compliance reports.

Requirements & Qualifications

  • Bachelor's degree or above in Psychology, Education, Social Sciences, IT Management, or related field.
  • Proven experience in project coordination, survey design, and data analysis is highly preferred.
  • Strong organizational, administrative, and interpersonal communication skills.
  • Excellent written and spoken English and Chinese (including Cantonese).
  • Genuine passion for promoting student mental health and resilience.
