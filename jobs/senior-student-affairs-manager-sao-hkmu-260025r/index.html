Duties & Responsibilities

  • Lead and develop a small team within the Student Affairs Office, fostering collaboration and accountability.
  • Provide strategic leadership for reviewing, enhancing and evaluating whole-person development schemes and mental health campaigns.
  • Oversee governance and management of student societies, advise student leaders, and ensure policy compliance.
  • Build effective partnerships across the University and with external stakeholders to advance student initiatives.
  • Drive evidence-informed innovation and continuous improvement in student affairs programmes with measurable outcomes.
  • Ensure effective planning and delivery of initiatives through project management, resource coordination and stakeholder communication.

Requirements & Qualifications

  • Recognised degree (preferably higher degree) with substantial administrative experience in tertiary education.
  • Substantial experience in student affairs with proven track record in developing and evaluating student-focused programmes.
  • Genuine commitment to whole-person development, student well-being and mental health promotion.
  • Excellent communication, interpersonal and people-management skills with ability to lead teams.
  • Strong project management and organisational skills with ability to manage multiple priorities.
  • Proficiency in English and Chinese (including Putonghua).
