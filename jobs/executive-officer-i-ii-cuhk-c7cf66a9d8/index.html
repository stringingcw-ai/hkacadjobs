Executive Officer I / II

Chinese University of Hong Kong

Department
Faculty of Law
Rank
Non-Academic
Position type
Full-time

Duties & Responsibilities

  • Provide administrative support to the Faculty of Law's academic programmes across multiple campuses.
  • Support all aspects of the new Executive Master of Laws in Global Practice Programme, including development, marketing, and operations.
  • Manage student admissions and interview processes.
  • Handle staff, student, and alumni communications to enhance student learning experience.
  • Perform committee work and liaise with internal departments, partners, and stakeholders.
  • Supervise staff and perform other assigned administrative tasks.
  • Travel outside Hong Kong occasionally as required.

Requirements & Qualifications

  • Hold a good Bachelor's degree.
  • Meet language requirements (visit https://www.hro.cuhk.edu.hk/en-gb/languageforeo).
  • Have at least 8 years' post-qualification executive experience (for Executive Officer I level).
  • Demonstrate excellent written and spoken English and Chinese (Cantonese and Putonghua).
  • Possess relevant administrative, managerial, or project experience.
  • Have strong project coordination and implementation skills.
  • Demonstrate ability to analyse operational needs and develop practical solutions.
  • Show strong interpersonal, communication, and organisational abilities.

Appointment

  • Contract — up to 2 years (with contract-end gratuity for 2-year contract), renewable subject to good performance and mutual agreement.
