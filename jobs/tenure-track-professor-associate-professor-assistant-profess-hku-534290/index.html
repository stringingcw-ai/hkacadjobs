Duties & Responsibilities

  • Contribute to teaching public health principles across multiple disciplines including public health, global health, medicine, nursing, and pharmacy.
  • Lead research programmes at the forefront of 21st century public health.
  • Mentor future academic leaders and teach students how to improve population health.
  • Respond to emerging priorities in human health as planetary, pathogenic, and behavioural changes evolve.

Requirements & Qualifications

  • Ph.D. degree or equivalent in a related discipline.
  • Evidence of exceptional public health scholarship demonstrated through leadership in publications and external grant support.
  • Commitment to developing knowledge relevant to improving population health and reducing health disparities.
  • Experience in teaching and mentoring academic leaders in public health contexts.
  • Preference for candidates using multidisciplinary approaches, advanced epidemiologic/experimental methods, or big data analytics in areas such as infectious diseases, cancer, cardiometabolic disease, mental health, or injury control.

Appointment

  • Full-time, three-year fixed-term contract with possibility of renewal and consideration for tenure before expiry of second three-year contract, subject to satisfactory performance. Exceptionally outstanding candidates may be considered for tenure-track appointment.
