Assistant Professor (Public History)

Chinese University of Hong Kong

Department
Department of History
Rank
Assistant Professor
Position type
Full-time
Application deadline
1 Oct 2026

Duties & Responsibilities

  • Teach an introductory methodology course in Public History and two courses per term (one course in the first term).
  • Conduct research with an active publication agenda in Public History.
  • Engage meaningfully with community, governmental, and non-governmental organizations.
  • Mentor and supervise students in their academic development.

Requirements & Qualifications

  • Hold a PhD in History or closely related field by August 1, 2027.
  • Demonstrate an active research agenda with clear plans for future publications.
  • Show commitment to teaching and mentoring students.
  • Provide evidence of public history practice and community engagement with demonstrated social impact.
  • Experience in Digital Humanities and innovative public-facing methodologies is highly desirable.
  • Knowledge of Chinese is advantageous; teaching conducted in English.

Appointment

  • Full-time, substantiation-track position (equivalent to U.S. tenure-track); initial contract of up to three years, potentially leading to longer-term appointment or substantiation after six years or earlier upon special application.

Key Dates

  • Start date: August 2, 2027
  • Closing date for PhD requirement: August 1, 2027
  • Review date: October 1, 2026 (applications reviewed and position filled on a rolling basis)
