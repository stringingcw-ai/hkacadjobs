Full-time/Part-time Research Assistant(s) / Junior Research Assistant(s)

Chinese University of Hong Kong

Department
Department of Ophthalmology and Visual Sciences
Rank
Research Assistant/Associate
Position type
Part-time

Duties & Responsibilities

  • Assist in clinical studies including literature reviews, data collection, management, and analysis.
  • Provide support for laboratory work such as specimen collection, processing, and storage.
  • Contribute to research programmes on glaucoma, diabetic retinopathy, and other eye diseases.
  • Perform shift duties on weekday evenings, weekends, and public holidays as required.

Requirements & Qualifications

  • Bachelor's degree in Chinese Medicine, Medicine, Public Health, Statistics, or related life science discipline.
  • Proficiency in English and Cantonese.
  • Keen interest in ophthalmology and visual sciences research.
  • Strong organisational, analytical, interpersonal, and problem-solving skills.
  • Self-motivation and ability to work independently and as part of a team.
  • Registration as Chinese Medicine Practitioner and relevant clinical/laboratory/statistical experience preferred.

Appointment

  • Full-time/Part-time — Contract for one to two years, commencing as soon as possible, renewable subject to mutual agreement and funding availability.
