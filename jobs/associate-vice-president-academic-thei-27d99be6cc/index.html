Duties & Responsibilities

  • Monitor implementation of the Institute's academic and operational plans.
  • Oversee academic programme management and development to meet industry needs.
  • Implement quality assurance mechanisms and enhance quality culture.
  • Devise new academic policies, regulations, and guidelines for institutional development.
  • Foster a productive learning environment and collegial work culture among academic units.
  • Manage applied research, scholarly, and consultancy operations.
  • Maximize student intakes through enhanced marketing and business development.

Requirements & Qualifications

  • Hold a recognized doctoral degree, preferably in a field relevant to the Institute's portfolio.
  • Demonstrate outstanding professional standing with strong industry networks and substantial scholarly achievements.
  • Have proven track record of management and academic leadership in higher education.
  • Possess solid experience in developing and enhancing quality educational, research, and employer-engagement activities.
  • Have rich experience in programme management, development, accreditation, and teaching strategies.
  • Demonstrate strong leadership, management, organizational, interpersonal, and communication skills.
  • Have high proficiency in spoken and written English and Chinese, preferably including Putonghua.

Appointment

  • Full-time; Rank of Professor; Based at Chai Wan Campus; May require work at different sites and outside Hong Kong, including irregular hours and weekend work.
