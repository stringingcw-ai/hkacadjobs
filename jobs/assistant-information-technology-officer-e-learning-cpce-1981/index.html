Duties & Responsibilities

  • Administer, configure and maintain the Learning Management System including course provisioning and access control.
  • Manage upload, version control and publication of course materials, teaching plans and assessment artefacts.
  • Provide e-learning support to academic staff and students and ensure online course readiness.
  • Set up Generative AI inference engine using local LLM with dataset preparation and fine-tuning.
  • Develop LLM and AI Agent applications using RAG, Multi-Agent and Agentic AI architectures.
  • Build data-processing and ETL pipelines for GenAI applications.

Requirements & Qualifications

  • Hold a recognised degree in Information Technology, Computing Studies or a related discipline.
  • Possess two years of relevant post-qualification experience.
  • Have experience designing and developing AI chatbots using local LLM.
  • Demonstrate knowledge of Python, HTML, CSS, Javascript, llama.cpp, LangChain, LangGraph, Unsloth, LanceDB, Neo4j, ZenML and Apache Airflow.
  • Possess good interpersonal and communication skills.

Appointment

  • Full-time contract — 2 years (with possibility of re-engagement subject to mutual agreement).

Key Dates

  • Consideration of applications commences: 10 July 2026.
