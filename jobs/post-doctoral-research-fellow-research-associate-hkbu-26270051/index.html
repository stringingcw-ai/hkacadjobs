# Post-Doctoral Research Fellow / Research Associate

Duties & Responsibilities

  • Provide scientific inputs and support for various research projects.
  • Conduct research and development work on translational medical research initiatives.
  • Manage project-related tasks and coordinate with the Principal Investigator.
  • Work primarily at the University's research facility in Hong Kong Science and Technology Parks.

Requirements & Qualifications

  • Hold a master's degree or above in immunology, virology, molecular biology, or related biological sciences from a recognised university.
  • Have at least 4 years of post-qualification research experience (Ph.D. holders may be considered for Post-doctoral Fellow position).
  • Demonstrate expertise in molecular cloning, vaccine antigen expression, cell culture assays, flow cytometry, and nanoparticle formulation.
  • Possess strong analytical and interpersonal skills with excellent attention to detail.
  • Demonstrate fluency in written and verbal English, Cantonese, and Putonghua.

Appointment

  • Fixed-term contract of two years.

Key Dates

  • Review date: 8 weeks after closing date (unsuccessful applicants notified).
  • Application deadline: Ongoing review until position is filled.
