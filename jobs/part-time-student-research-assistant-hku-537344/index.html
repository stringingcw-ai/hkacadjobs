Duties & Responsibilities

  • Accompany research team members to elderly care homes and community centres to conduct field tests of a multimodal recommendation application.
  • Assist elderly participants and users in testing the application and explain testing procedures in Cantonese and Mandarin.
  • Communicate patiently with elderly participants and provide assistance during application use.
  • Collect user feedback, survey responses, and research data during field tests.
  • Record issues encountered by participants and report them to the research team.
  • Assist with coordination and organisation of field-testing activities and support other research tasks as assigned.

Requirements & Qualifications

  • Must be a current undergraduate student at the university, able to work part-time.
  • Fluency in both Cantonese and Mandarin is essential.
  • Patience, willingness to communicate with elderly participants, and a responsible, friendly, respectful attitude.
  • Good interpersonal and communication skills with sufficient availability and flexibility for field visits.
  • Ability to follow research protocols, handle participant information responsibly, and work effectively with research team members.
  • Previous experience in volunteering, elderly care, community service, user studies, surveys, or field research is advantageous but not required.

Appointment

  • Part-time, temporary with possibility of renewal subject to satisfactory performance and funding availability.
