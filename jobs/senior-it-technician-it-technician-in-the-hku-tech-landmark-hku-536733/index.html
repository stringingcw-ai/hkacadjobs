Duties & Responsibilities

  • Oversee full-lifecycle rollout, maintenance, and technical support for office automation systems and facility booking platforms.
  • Deliver specialist technical guidance and round-the-clock support for PC and server software/hardware deployment, backups, updates, and disaster recovery.
  • Provide comprehensive AV and IT technical support to facilitate research and teaching activities.
  • Ensure full compliance with Information Security & Data Management governance standards.

Requirements & Qualifications

  • Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, Electronic Engineering, or related discipline.
  • Minimum 5 years of full-time professional experience in IT management, technical support, and system solution deployment.
  • Expertise in office automation systems, digital booking/charging systems, user account administration, and network infrastructure management.
  • Hands-on proficiency in virtualisation platforms (VMware ESXi, Hyper-V) and experience with P2V migration and virtual machine lifecycle management.
  • Experience in web application development, database design, administration, performance tuning, and system integration.
  • Fluency in written and spoken English and Chinese.
  • Industry certifications (Microsoft Certified Systems Engineer, Windows Server Hybrid Administrator Associate, JNTCP-EX, CCNA, Crestron) are advantageous.

Appointment

  • Full-time, two-year fixed-term contract with possibility of renewal subject to funding availability and satisfactory performance; includes contract-end gratuity and University retirement benefits contribution up to 10% of basic salary.

  • Commencement: As soon as possible
