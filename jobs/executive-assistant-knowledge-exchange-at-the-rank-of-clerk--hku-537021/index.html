Duties & Responsibilities

  • Provide administrative, clerical, operational, and coordination support for the Faculty's Knowledge Exchange Team and its daily operations.
  • Assist with compliance matters, project administration, and related Knowledge Exchange initiatives and projects.
  • Support research centres, institutes, and cross-functional collaboration with other departments.
  • Perform other responsibilities as assigned by Faculty Management and the Director.

Requirements & Qualifications

  • Hold a good bachelor's degree with minimum 2 years' post-qualification work experience (fresh graduates with strong academic background may be considered).
  • Demonstrate good command of written and spoken English and Chinese.
  • Possess strong organizational, interpersonal, and IT skills with attention to detail.
  • Show ability to multitask, prioritize, work independently and in teams, and perform under pressure.
  • Experience in tertiary institutions or the education sector is advantageous.

Appointment

  • Full-time, two-year fixed-term contract with possibility of renewal subject to performance and mutual agreement; includes contract-end gratuity and up to 10% University contribution to retirement benefits scheme.
