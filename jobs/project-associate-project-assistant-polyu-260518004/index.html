Duties & Responsibilities

  • Assist the project leader in operating the Joint Chinese Textile Centre between PolyU and the Hong Kong Palace Museum.
  • Design, develop, and maintain a structured database for exhibits from the Chris Hall collection and other museum holdings.
  • Conduct regular data entry, validation, and updating to ensure database accuracy and completeness.
  • Liaise with project leaders, curators, and technical staff to identify data needs and improve database functionality.
  • Support data collection and organisation for research and documentation purposes.
  • Assist in drafting procedural guidelines for data management and database usage.
  • Perform additional duties as assigned by the project leader or Dean of School.

Requirements & Qualifications

  • Master's degree in Humanities (preferably Chinese History, Cultural History, or Art History) OR honours degree in these fields with 3+ years of relevant work experience for Project Associate role.
  • Honours degree in Humanities fields OR equivalent qualification with 2+ years of relevant work experience for Project Assistant role.
  • Knowledge of and experience with academic and museum databases and library resources.
  • Experience in data entry with strong ability to search, collect, and organise research materials.
  • Good command of written and spoken English and Chinese.
