Duties & Responsibilities

  • Conduct classroom-based ethnographic data collection focusing on diversity-oriented teaching practices.
  • Liaise with local Hong Kong schools to facilitate research activities.
  • Collect, manage, and analyze research data using mixed-methods approaches.
  • Provide administrative and editorial support to the project team.
  • Prepare research reports and publications.
  • Coordinate workshops and seminars related to the project.

Requirements & Qualifications

  • Hold at least a Bachelor's degree or equivalent qualification.
  • Demonstrate expertise in qualitative observational data collection and analysis.
  • Possess strong command of spoken Cantonese and Mandarin with excellent English academic writing skills.
  • Be mature, open-minded, and possess strong interpersonal skills and intercultural sensitivity.
  • Work independently with strong attention to detail and ability to manage multiple tasks.
  • Preference given to candidates with MPhil/doctoral degree, anthropology/teacher education background, and experience working with primary and secondary school teachers and students.

  • Full-time — approximately 15 months
