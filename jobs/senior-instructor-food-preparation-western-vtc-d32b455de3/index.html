Duties & Responsibilities

  • Conduct classes and practical training in western food preparation.
  • Develop programme curriculum and teaching, learning and assessment materials.
  • Deliver catering and culinary services during daily operations.
  • Supervise staff and resources, prepare financial estimates and maintain records.
  • Handle student and academic administration including recruitment and placement.
  • Oversee procurement, maintenance and commissioning of equipment.
  • Manage training programmes and daily kitchen operations.
  • Liaise with industry counterparts and related associations.

Requirements & Qualifications

  • Higher diploma in Hotel/Catering Management/Culinary Arts or relevant discipline; preferably Certified Trainer Chef Certificate in Western Cuisine.
  • At least 6 years of relevant work or teaching experience in hotel/catering industry.
  • Good supervisory experience and administrative abilities.
  • Good knowledge of trade-specific terminologies.
  • Good communication and presentation skills in English and Chinese; Putonghua knowledge is preferable.
  • Good knowledge of basic computer applications.
  • Ability to pass a trade test.
