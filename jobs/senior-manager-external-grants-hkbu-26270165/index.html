Duties & Responsibilities

  • Provide strategic and operational leadership for the University's external research-funding portfolio and establish annual priorities and performance measures.
  • Oversee the full lifecycle of external grants from opportunity scanning through project closure, including compliance checks and award acceptance.
  • Lead and develop staff managing RGC schemes, Mainland grants, fellowship schemes and other sponsor portfolios.
  • Interpret sponsor requirements, resolve complex cases and maintain relationships with funding bodies, faculties and partner institutions.
  • Use portfolio data and funding intelligence to strengthen application quality and institutional competitiveness.

Requirements & Qualifications

  • Bachelor's degree or above, preferably with a postgraduate qualification.
  • At least 8 years of relevant professional experience in research administration or grant management in higher education, with substantial supervisory or managerial experience.
  • Thorough knowledge of Hong Kong's research-funding landscape, including RGC and ITC schemes.
  • Proven leadership, project-management, analytical and problem-solving skills with ability to manage competing priorities.
  • Excellent stakeholder-management and communication skills with confidence to liaise with senior academics.
  • Excellent command of written and spoken English and Chinese, including Putonghua.
  • Strong digital literacy and ability to use research-information systems, data analytics and AI tools effectively.

Appointment

  • Full-time fixed-term contract for 3 years; re-appointment subject to mutual agreement.

Key Dates

  • Interview decision deadline: 8 weeks after application submission.
