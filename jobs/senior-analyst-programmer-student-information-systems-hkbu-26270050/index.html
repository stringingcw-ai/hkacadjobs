Duties & Responsibilities

  • Develop, maintain, and support Student Information Systems and related digital transformation projects.
  • Assist supervisor in IT project implementation, vendor collaboration, and consultant coordination.
  • Conduct feasibility studies, proof-of-concepts, testing, training, and deployment for business requirements.
  • Work independently on application development including system design, coding, and implementation for PowerBuilder and web-based solutions.
  • Monitor systems proactively, identify and resolve application issues to ensure optimal performance.
  • Collaborate with IT teams, vendors, and consultants to deliver efficient project outcomes.

Requirements & Qualifications

  • Bachelor's degree in information technology, computer science, or related discipline.
  • Minimum 4 years post-qualification experience in application development.
  • Proficiency in Java/JEE development including EJB, JSF, AJAX, Spring Boot, RESTful APIs, React, and Node.js.
  • Frontend and web development skills in HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and PHP.
  • Database development experience with SQL, PL/SQL, and RDBMS systems (Sybase, MySQL, MSSQL).
  • Experience administering IBM WebSphere Server and Oracle WebLogic Server.
  • Strong self-starter, results-oriented mindset with business acumen.
  • Ability to work independently and collaboratively in team environments.
  • API management tools experience (e.g., Boomi) is advantageous; student information systems experience is highly desirable.

Appointment

  • Full-time; Fixed-term contract of two and a half years.
