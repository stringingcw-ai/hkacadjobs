Duties & Responsibilities

  • Conduct research on GenAI Agents development and applications.
  • Support the Center Director with daily lab-office administration of the Enterprise Lab.
  • Organize research seminars, workshops, and academic-industrial events including planning, logistics, and reporting.
  • Facilitate Enterprise Lab R&D by coordinating with faculty, researchers, and industry partners.
  • Serve as a liaison to existing sponsors and help sustain collaborations.
  • Identify and recruit new industrial sponsors through proposals and value proposition presentations.
  • Assist with research funding applications and coordinate industry-sourced sponsorships.
  • Manage documentation and reporting including contracts, agreements, and records.
  • Provide support in budgeting and purchase order processing.
  • Track event and partnership outcomes and recommend improvements.

Requirements & Qualifications

  • Bachelor's degree or equivalent in engineering, management, or a related field required.
  • Demonstrated coordination experience in academic or research environments with industry links.
  • Strong organizational and multi-tasking skills to manage multiple projects simultaneously.
  • Good command of written and spoken English and Chinese; Putonghua proficiency advantageous.
  • Experience in stakeholder engagement, sponsorship recruitment, and relationship management.
  • Familiarity with research funding applications and academic research environments desirable.
  • Competency in office software applications such as Microsoft Office Suite.
