Duties & Responsibilities

  • Conduct research independently with the Principal Investigator on educational topics.
  • Perform systematic reviews of literature and focus-group interviews in multicultural school settings.
  • Develop study designs and oversee project timelines and research outcomes.
  • Manage data collection, analysis, interpretation, and prepare research reports and papers.
  • Train research assistants and perform other assigned duties.

Requirements & Qualifications

  • Hold a Master's degree in Psychology, Neuroscience, Cognitive Science or equivalent with 3 years' related work experience, or a Ph.D. in a relevant discipline.
  • Possess solid statistical and computing skills.
  • Demonstrate effective written and oral communication in Chinese (mainly Cantonese) and English.
  • Ability to work independently and collaboratively to conduct educational research.
  • Research experience in educational settings is advantageous.
