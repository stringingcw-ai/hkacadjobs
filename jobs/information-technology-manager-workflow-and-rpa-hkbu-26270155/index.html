Duties & Responsibilities

  • Design, develop, and manage automated workflow solutions to streamline administrative and academic operations.
  • Architect and implement workflow with RPA applications to enhance operational efficiency and productivity.
  • Collaborate with academic and administrative units to identify digital transformation opportunities and deliver tailored solutions.
  • Oversee end-to-end project lifecycles, manage external vendors and system integrators, and ensure timely delivery.
  • Serve as subject matter expert, supervising internal technical teams and external consultants on system design and deployment.
  • Engage business users to collect functional requirements and translate them into actionable user stories.

Requirements & Qualifications

  • Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Information Systems, or related disciplines.
  • Minimum 7 years of proven experience in IT application development.
  • Demonstrated success in developing and managing workflow automation applications using workflow engines such as ServiceNow.
  • Strong leadership skills with ability to manage and guide a small team.
  • Capability to translate business requirements into technical specifications and user stories.
  • Solid project management skills, including vendor coordination and oversight.
  • Excellent communication and interpersonal skills with academic and administrative stakeholders.
  • Ability to work well in team environments and independently.

Appointment

  • Full-time, fixed-term contract of 2 years; re-appointment subject to performance review, mutual agreement, and funding availability.

Key Dates

  • Review date: 8 weeks after application submission (unsuccessful candidates will be notified if not invited for interview).
