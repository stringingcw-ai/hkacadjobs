Computer Technician II

Chinese University of Hong Kong

Department
Zhizhen School of Interdisciplinary Mathematical Sciences
Rank
Non-Academic
Position type
Full-time

Duties & Responsibilities

  • Perform daily inspection, maintenance, fault analysis, and handling of computer servers.
  • Configure and manage IT infrastructure and equipment including firewalls and networking devices.
  • Provide technical support and troubleshooting assistance to end-users.
  • Oversee IT equipment inventory and ensure proper maintenance.
  • Maintain and update the school's websites and social media.
  • Provide occasional on-site technical support during non-office hours.

Requirements & Qualifications

  • Hold a Diploma/Certificate from a recognised institution, preferably a Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Information Engineering, or related field.
  • Demonstrate proficiency in computer networking, office automation, security incident handling, and server room setup.
  • Possess excellent communication and presentation skills.
  • Have strong command of written and spoken English and Chinese including Putonghua.
  • Exhibit strong problem-solving, troubleshooting and organisational skills.
  • Be self-motivated with a strong sense of responsibility and ability to work independently and collaboratively with flexibility and creativity.

Appointment

  • Full-time, 2-year contract (renewable)
