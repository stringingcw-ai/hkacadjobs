Duties & Responsibilities

  • Assist the Principal Investigator and Co-investigators in conducting research tasks on a psychosocial study of students with special educational needs.
  • Review literature, conduct mixed-method research, and coordinate with team members and collaborating partners.
  • Collect and analyze quantitative and qualitative data, conduct in-depth interviews, and compile research reports.
  • Write peer-reviewed journal articles and supervise research assistants.

Requirements & Qualifications

  • Hold a Master's degree or above in Social Sciences, Applied Psychology, Statistics, Computer Science, or Data Science.
  • Have at least five years of relevant research experience in quantitative and qualitative methods.
  • Be familiar with randomized controlled trial and mixed-methods research procedures.
  • Possess knowledge of SPSS and NVivo software, with statistical modeling knowledge as an advantage.
  • Work independently with strong analytical skills, attention to detail, and ability to produce clear research outputs.
  • Have excellent communication skills and fluency in both English and Chinese.
