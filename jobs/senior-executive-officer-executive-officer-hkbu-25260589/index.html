Duties & Responsibilities

  • Provide administrative and logistical support for project implementation across clinical, research, and administrative units.
  • Coordinate meetings, training sessions, pilot study logistics, and stakeholder engagement activities.
  • Prepare and maintain documentation including SOP drafts, minutes, evidence summaries, and evaluation reports.
  • Support organisation and record-keeping of different working groups.
  • Assist in procurement processes, budgeting, and audit preparation.
  • Communicate with internal and external stakeholders including experts, clinical teams, and partner institutions.
  • Support monitoring and reporting activities throughout implementation and updating phases.

Requirements & Qualifications

  • Hold a recognised university degree in any discipline.
  • Possess minimum 2–3 years of relevant administrative experience, ideally in healthcare, education, or research settings.
  • Demonstrate strong organisational, multitasking, and communication skills.
  • Have excellent command of spoken and written English and Chinese, including Putonghua.
  • Experience in administration or project coordination is preferred.
  • Work experience in clinical pathway development is highly desirable.
  • Experience supporting large-scale projects, training logistics, or clinical documentation is an advantage.

Appointment

  • Full-time; location at The Chinese Medicine Hospital of Hong Kong, Tseung Kwan O; weekends and/or public holidays work required; candidates with less experience will be considered as Executive Officer.
