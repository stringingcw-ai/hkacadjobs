Duties & Responsibilities

  • Manage and analyse large-scale clinical datasets including electronic health records and surgical registry data.
  • Apply statistical modelling techniques such as multivariable regression, survival analysis, and propensity score methods to clinical research questions.
  • Develop and implement machine learning and AI algorithms for outcome prediction and risk stratification.
  • Conduct systematic literature reviews and meta-analyses for grant-funded projects.
  • Assist in preparing manuscripts, conference presentations, grant proposals, and ethics submissions.
  • Maintain research databases while ensuring data integrity and regulatory compliance.

Requirements & Qualifications

  • Bachelor's degree or above in Statistics, Biostatistics, Data Science, Computer Science, or related discipline.
  • Proficiency in at least two programming languages: R, Python, Stata, or SAS.
  • Experience handling large and complex datasets including data cleaning and linkage.
  • Good written and spoken English; Chinese language proficiency preferred.
  • Strong analytical, organisational, and interpersonal skills.
  • Master's degree or clinical research experience is advantageous; machine learning framework experience is highly desirable.

Appointment

  • Full-time contract — either one year or two years fixed-term (duration to be confirmed after interview); possibility of renewal subject to satisfactory performance and funding availability.
