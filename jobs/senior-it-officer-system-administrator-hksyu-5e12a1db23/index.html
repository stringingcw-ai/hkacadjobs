Duties & Responsibilities

  • Manage, design, implement, and maintain the university's IT infrastructure including virtual machines, servers, and storage systems.
  • Oversee administrative tasks for Active Directory, DNS, Linux, email systems, and database servers.
  • Address security vulnerabilities through system hardening and patch management.
  • Manage incident response processes and develop disaster recovery plans.
  • Conduct product evaluations and prepare specifications for new deployments.
  • Plan, implement, and maintain server rooms and data centers.
  • Perform daily operational health checks and monitor IT systems proactively.
  • Create and maintain technical documentation and user manuals for IT personnel.

Requirements & Qualifications

  • Hold a Higher Diploma or Degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, or related field.
  • Have at least 3 years of experience in Windows infrastructure, VMware, Active Directory, and Linux.
  • Be proficient in Linux, web-based open-source environments, Windows PowerShell, and Linux Shell scripting.
  • Possess RHCE, LPI, or MCSE certification (preferred).
  • Demonstrate strong analytical and troubleshooting skills.
  • Be fluent in both spoken and written English and Chinese.
  • Work effectively as a team player and independently during incidents.
  • Be familiar with system security and data protection best practices.

Appointment

  • Full-time contract position, renewable upon mutual agreement. (Candidates with more experience may be considered for Assistant IT Manager role.)
