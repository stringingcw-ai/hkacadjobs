Duties & Responsibilities

  • Conduct literature reviews and recruit research participants for data collection from South Asian communities.
  • Perform in-depth interviews and qualitative data analysis, then compile research reports.
  • Prepare materials for conference presentations and publications.
  • Handle administrative tasks and perform other duties as assigned.
  • Work irregular hours and weekends as needed for data collection activities.

Requirements & Qualifications

  • Hold a recognized bachelor's degree or above in social sciences or a relevant discipline.
  • Have at least one year of relevant work experience.
  • Possess a strong network within South Asian communities.
  • Be familiar with in-depth interview procedures and qualitative data analysis methods.
  • Demonstrate excellent written and spoken English and Chinese skills.
  • Show strong communication, organizational, and interpersonal abilities with responsibility and self-motivation.
