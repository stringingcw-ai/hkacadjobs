Duties & Responsibilities

  • Manage and maintain access to subscribed and open-access electronic resources including setup, activation and maintenance.
  • Coordinate electronic resource lifecycle activities such as trials, renewals and changes with vendors and stakeholders.
  • Monitor and resolve access and discovery issues in collaboration with vendors and Library teams.
  • Maintain and improve electronic resource records and knowledge base settings for accurate discovery.
  • Collect, analyse and report usage statistics to support evidence-based decision-making.
  • Develop, document and improve team workflows and procedures for electronic resource management.
  • Collaborate with Library teams to support user-oriented discovery services.

Requirements & Qualifications

  • Recognized degree or diploma in Library and Information Science or related discipline.
  • 10 years relevant post-qualification experience preferred.
  • Proficiency in written and spoken English and Chinese, including Putonghua.
  • In-depth knowledge of electronic resources management, including activation and issue resolution.
  • Familiarity with Alma and PrimoVE systems and knowledge base/link resolver concepts.
  • Experience managing electronic resource records and ensuring accurate discovery and coverage.
  • Strong communication, interpersonal, analytical and problem-solving skills.
