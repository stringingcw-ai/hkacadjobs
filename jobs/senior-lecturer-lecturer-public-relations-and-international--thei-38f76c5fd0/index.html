# Senior Lecturer / Lecturer (Public Relations and International Events Management)

Duties & Responsibilities

  • Teach and develop curriculum for the Public Relations and International Events Management programme in English and/or Putonghua.
  • Support student learning and supervise Final Year Projects.
  • Participate in academic and scholarly activities of the department.
  • Provide academic and administrative support services for the programme.

Requirements & Qualifications

  • Hold a recognised Master's degree (preferably Doctoral degree) in International Events Management, Marketing Research, Customer Relationship Management, Public Relations, Communication, or related fields.
  • Have at least 3 years of tertiary-level teaching experience and/or professional practice in relevant fields.
  • Demonstrate strong interpersonal and communication skills with high proficiency in English and Putonghua.
  • Meet one of four criteria for Putonghua proficiency (mother tongue, degree through Putonghua MOI, relevant work experience, or formal language qualification).

Appointment

  • Full-time or Part-time / Short-term contract basis — duration not specified
