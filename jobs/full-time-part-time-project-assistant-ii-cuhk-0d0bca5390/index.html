Full-time / Part-time Project Assistant II

Chinese University of Hong Kong

Department
Centre for Entrepreneurship
Rank
Non-Academic
Position type
Part-time

Duties & Responsibilities

  • Provide logistics and operational support to competitions and events organised by the Centre.
  • Prepare online and offline publicity materials.
  • Handle daily correspondence, invoices, payments, documents and filing.
  • Assist in administrative work of similar entrepreneurial projects within the Centre.
  • Perform ad hoc duties and other tasks as assigned.

Requirements & Qualifications

  • Completed secondary education with Grade E/Level 2 or above in at least 5 subjects including Chinese and English.
  • Bachelor's degree.
  • Fast and accurate Chinese and English word-processing skills.
  • At least 1 year's post-qualification work experience, preferably in administration or project co-ordination.
  • Good command of written and spoken English and Chinese (including Putonghua).
  • Good interpersonal and communication skills.
  • Self-motivated and mature work attitude with good work ethics.
  • Ability to work independently.
  • Proficiency in MS Word, Excel, PowerPoint and Canva.
  • Experience in event administration is advantageous.

Appointment

  • Full-time contract — 1 year, renewable subject to good performance and mutual agreement.
