Assistant Engineer (Operation Maintenance Services)

Duties & Responsibilities

  • Lead a team of frontline staff and manage contractors for building services and maintenance across all campuses.
  • Perform day-to-day electrical and mechanical plant operations, including routine maintenance and emergency repairs.
  • Prepare and execute routine and preventive maintenance plans with regular site supervision.
  • Manage system breakdowns to minimize operational downtime and impact.
  • Conduct site inspections for repair, fitting-out, upgrade and improvement works.
  • Liaise with end users and contractors on installation and maintenance projects.
  • Monitor system performance and plant efficiency.
  • Assist with ad hoc tasks as assigned by senior staff.

Requirements & Qualifications

  • Completed secondary education with eight years' relevant post-qualification experience, or higher diploma with substantial relevant experience.
  • Valid Registered Electrical Worker Grade A or above.
  • Knowledge of building services engineering and experience in design, installation, operation and maintenance.
  • Hands-on experience in operating and maintaining building services and systems.
  • Previous experience in electrical repair and maintenance highly preferred.
  • Good communication, interpersonal and presentation skills.

Appointment

  • Fixed-term contract of two years with re-appointment subject to performance review, mutual agreement and funding availability.
