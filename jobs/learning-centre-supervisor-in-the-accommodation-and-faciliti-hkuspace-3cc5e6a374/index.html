Duties & Responsibilities

  • Lead and coach support staff in daily operations, school events, and functions.
  • Manage enrolment, enquiry services, and facility operations of learning centres.
  • Supervise setup, maintenance, and upkeep of teaching equipment and venue arrangements.
  • Coordinate renovation projects and repairs of equipment, furniture, and school premises.
  • Provide administrative support to academic and administrative units.
  • Conduct team briefings for task allocation and service enhancement.
  • Assist with staff training, deployment, and duty roster preparation.
  • Monitor procurement, inventory, and performance of service contractors.
  • Prepare statistics and regular reports; shift duties including weekends and public holidays may be required.

Requirements & Qualifications

  • Achieve Level 2 or above in five subjects in HKDSE (or equivalent qualification).
  • Meet Level 2 or above in both Chinese and English language proficiency.
  • Hold a Bachelor's degree (advantageous).
  • Possess 6 years of related working experience with at least 2 years at supervisory level.
  • Demonstrate strong leadership, organisational, and interpersonal skills.
  • Exhibit proficiency in Microsoft Office and Chinese word processing.
  • Have experience managing service contractors and suppliers.
  • Show ability to multitask and work independently under time pressure.
  • Experience with AI tools and applications is advantageous.

Appointment

  • Full-time contract — 2 years with possibility of renewal subject to review; includes contract-end gratuity, medical and dental benefits, 18 days' annual leave, and staff development opportunities.
