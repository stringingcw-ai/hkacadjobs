Duties & Responsibilities

  • Provide support to a learning and teaching project on redesigning learning outcomes and assessments in the context of generative AI.
  • Prepare and develop research and training materials for the project.
  • Conduct literature reviews, data collection and analysis, documentation, and report writing.
  • Organize meetings and workshops related to the project.
  • Perform other research-related duties as assigned by the Principal Investigator or delegates.

Requirements & Qualifications

  • Hold a Bachelor's degree in Education or a relevant discipline.
  • Have excellent command of written and spoken English and Chinese (including Cantonese).
  • Demonstrate self-motivation, strong organizational skills, and good interpersonal and communication abilities.
  • Ability to work independently and collaboratively as an effective team member.
  • Familiarity with human-AI interaction or generative AI in education is advantageous but not required.
