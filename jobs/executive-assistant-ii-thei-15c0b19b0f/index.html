Duties & Responsibilities

  • Perform administrative and support duties for the President's Office.
  • Handle sourcing, ordering, stock management of supplies, and asset management.
  • Assist with document and records management, including data entry.
  • Organize and support meetings, events, conferences, and other institutional activities.
  • Coordinate campus visits and tours, and provide logistical support for external guests.
  • Draft official correspondence including emails, letters, agendas, and meeting notes.
  • Handle enquiries from internal and external parties.
  • Prepare presentation slides, posters, and event materials.

Requirements & Qualifications

  • Hold Level 2 or above in 5 subjects (including English, Chinese, and Mathematics) in HKDSE or equivalent qualification.
  • Demonstrate politeness, attention to detail, organization, proactivity, and flexibility.
  • Handle confidential and sensitive information appropriately.
  • Possess high proficiency in MS Word, Excel, PowerPoint, and Chinese word processing.
  • Have strong interpersonal and communication skills in English and Chinese; Putonghua fluency is advantageous.
  • Pass a written test and computer skills test if shortlisted.
