Duties & Responsibilities

  • Lead a team of building professionals managing campus development projects across capital and non-capital works.
  • Manage project implementation including design, contract management, construction administration, quality control and handover.
  • Formulate Campus Master Plan and identify opportunities for physical environment improvements.
  • Provide space planning, feasibility studies, and design support to academic and non-academic units.
  • Manage space inventory and smart space management systems across campuses.
  • Coordinate with end-user departments to identify spatial needs and ensure minimal operational disruption.
  • Lead multidisciplinary teams of external consultants and internal managers to deliver projects on time, budget, and quality targets.
  • Provide supervision and mentorship to team members throughout all project stages.
  • Oversee coordination with Cost and Contract Management Section on procurement, contracts, and budgets.
  • Identify project risks and support development of contingency and mitigation plans.

Requirements & Qualifications

  • Recognised professional degree in Architecture with minimum 8 years of post-qualification work experience.
  • Strong written and spoken English and Chinese communication skills.
  • Proficiency in AutoCAD, Photoshop, and SketchUp; Revit is advantageous.
  • Ability to manage multiple projects in dynamic environments with service-oriented mindset.
  • Shortlisted candidates must sit for a written test.

Appointment

  • Full-time, fixed-term contract of two years; re-appointment subject to performance review, mutual agreement, and funding availability.
