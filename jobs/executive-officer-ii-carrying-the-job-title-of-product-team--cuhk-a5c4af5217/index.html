Executive Officer II (carrying the job title of ‘Product Team Lead’)

Chinese University of Hong Kong

Department
Department of Psychology
Rank
Non-Academic
Position type
Full-time

Duties & Responsibilities

  • Own the end-to-end product lifecycle for digital mental health platforms from ideation to launch and optimization.
  • Define product vision, strategy, and roadmap with the Principal Investigator and internal teams.
  • Collaborate with external tech vendors to manage development processes for new features.
  • Translate user problems and business requirements into detailed product specifications and success metrics.
  • Conduct sprint planning, backlog grooming, and requirement reviews with technical teams.
  • Prioritize product backlog based on user needs, business objectives, and technical feasibility.
  • Conduct user research and analysis to understand user needs and pain points.
  • Track and analyze product performance metrics to identify improvement areas.

Requirements & Qualifications

  • Bachelor's degree in Business, Psychology, or related discipline.
  • At least 4 years' executive experience in product ownership and team management.
  • Proven track record of successfully launching and managing consumer Web and App digital products.
  • Experience writing technical requirements, user stories, and product requirements documents.
  • Exceptional leadership skills in managing cross-functional teams of designers and engineers.
  • Strong communication and stakeholder management skills.
  • Strong organizational skills with ability to manage multiple parallel workstreams.
