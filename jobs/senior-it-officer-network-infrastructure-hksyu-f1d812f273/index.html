Duties & Responsibilities

  • Manage, implement, and maintain the university's network infrastructure including computers, firewalls, switches, servers, and telecommunications.
  • Perform network administration tasks such as troubleshooting, configuration, deployment, and disaster recovery planning.
  • Plan and manage network infrastructure upgrades including replacement and enhancement of switches and firewalls.
  • Manage, monitor, and expand the university's Wi-Fi network.
  • Prepare network diagrams and technical documentation for servers, systems, and infrastructure.
  • Conduct daily health checks and monitor IT systems to ensure timely service restoration during disruptions.
  • Carry out scheduled maintenance or upgrades outside regular office hours when necessary.

Requirements & Qualifications

  • Higher Diploma or above in IT or related disciplines required.
  • At least 5 years of relevant experience preferred.
  • Solid experience with LAN/WAN, switches, firewalls, routers, and wireless networks.
  • Strong analytical and independent problem-solving skills with ability to work under pressure.
  • Self-motivated with ability to collaborate with IT team and users.
  • Good interpersonal and communication skills.
