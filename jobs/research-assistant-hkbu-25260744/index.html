Duties & Responsibilities

  • Conduct literature review and synthesize findings in financial disclosure, media monitoring and shares ownership information.
  • Collect and collate research data on market capitalization, ownership structure, governance indicators and social capital network mapping.
  • Assist in designing research methodologies to collect, organize and analyze qualitative and quantitative data.
  • Prepare qualitative research data and compile statistical reports.
  • Maintain accurate audit trail records and ensure compliance with research ethical guidelines.

Requirements & Qualifications

  • Bachelor's degree with some work experience for Research Assistant position; or Bachelor's degree with 3+ years' work experience OR Master's degree for Senior Research Assistant position.
  • Master's degree in Financial Journalism, Accounting, Business Analytics, Business Management or Finance is highly preferred.
  • Prior research experience in data collection, news monitoring and sentiment analysis of business news and financial disclosures is highly preferred.
  • Experience in research project execution including research design, data collection methodology, qualitative interpretation and basic statistical analysis.
  • Proficiency in data collection tools such as NVivo, Wisers, Meltwater and other commonly used programmes.

Appointment

  • Part-time (Senior Research Assistant or Research Assistant position)
