Student Housing Officer

Hong Kong Baptist University

Department
Office of Student Affairs
Rank

Duties & Responsibilities

  • Support planning, implementation, and evaluation of residential education programmes and activities.
  • Coordinate with student leaders to deliver residential education initiatives.
  • Engage in resident communication and encourage student participation in residential life.
  • Prepare regular statistics and reports for senior management.
  • Provide administrative and operational support to hostel management and development.
  • Work outside normal office hours on a need basis.

Requirements & Qualifications

  • At least 3 years of relevant work experience, preferably in tertiary institutions or student residence settings.
  • Genuine interest in student development with strong organisational and people management skills.
  • Excellent interpersonal and communication skills, fluent in written and spoken English and Chinese (Cantonese and Putonghua).
  • Strong sense of responsibility, sound judgement, and problem-solving ability.
  • Proficiency in graphic design software, video editing software, standard office applications, and information systems.

Appointment

  • Full-time, two-year fixed-term contract with possible reappointment subject to mutual agreement and satisfactory performance.
