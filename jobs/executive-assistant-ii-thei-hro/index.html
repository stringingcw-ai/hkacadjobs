Executive Assistant II

Technological and Higher Education Institute of Hong Kong

Department
Human Resources Office
Rank
Non-Academic
Position type
Full-time
Reference
HRO

Duties & Responsibilities

  • Provide secretarial and administrative support to the Human Resources Office.
  • Support HR functions including recruitment, onboarding, offboarding, and compensation & benefits.
  • Maintain accurate employee databases and filing records.
  • Draft correspondence such as emails, letters, and notices.
  • Assist in HR document management and compile management reports and statistics.
  • Source, order, and manage office supplies stock.
  • Provide logistical support for boards, meetings, and staff activities.

Requirements & Qualifications

  • Hold HKDSE Level 2 or above in 5 subjects (including English, Chinese, and Mathematics) or equivalent qualification.
  • Demonstrate proficiency in MS Word, MS Excel, MS PowerPoint, and Chinese word processing.
  • Possess good communication, interpersonal, and organisational skills.
  • Have strong command of English and Chinese, preferably including Putonghua.
  • Be flexible, polite, detail-minded, organised, and self-initiated.
  • Demonstrate confidentiality, integrity, and ability to work independently and as a team player.

Appointment

  • Full-time
  • Appointee may work irregular hours or shifts and perform duties on Saturdays/Sundays as necessary.
  • Appointee will work at the Chai Wan Campus.
  • Remuneration will be competitive and commensurate with qualifications and experience.
  • Shortlisted applicants must attend and pass a computer skills test.
