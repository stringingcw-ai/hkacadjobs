Duties & Responsibilities

  • Maintain and update files and databases.

Requirements & Qualifications

  • At least Grade E/Level 2 or equivalent in five HKCEE or HKDSEE subjects including English Language, Chinese Language and Mathematics.
  • Five years of clerical working experience.
  • Good communication skills in English, Chinese and Putonghua.
