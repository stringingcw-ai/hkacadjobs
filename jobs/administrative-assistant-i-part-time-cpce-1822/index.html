Duties & Responsibilities

  • Provide clerical assistance in admissions, registrations, examinations, and student financial assistance matters.
  • Assist in various functions, events, and activities.
  • Support counter operations and handle telephone enquiries.
  • Maintain and update files and databases.
  • Liaise with students, internal staff, and external organisations.
  • Undertake service counter duties on weekdays and/or weekends during daytime and evening hours.

Requirements & Qualifications

  • Achieve at least Grade E/Level 2 or equivalent in five HKCEE or HKDSEE subjects, including English Language (Grade C minimum for Syllabus A), Chinese Language, and Mathematics.
  • Have five years of clerical working experience.
  • Demonstrate good communication skills in English, Chinese, and Putonghua.
  • Have good command of MS Office tools (Word, Excel, and PowerPoint).
  • Applicants with higher qualifications but less experience will be considered.

Appointment

  • Part-time; contract duration not specified.

  • Consideration of applications commences: 30 April 2026 (until positions are filled).
