Duties & Responsibilities

  • Hold a recognized undergraduate degree in Chinese Medicine registrable with the Chinese Medicine Council of Hong Kong and a Ph.D. in Chinese Medicine or related disciplines.
  • Demonstrate substantial experience in Chinese medicine clinical practice, teaching, and course development in tertiary institutions.
  • Show a proven track record of high-quality research with impactful publications, external grants, postgraduate supervision, and cross-disciplinary collaborations.
  • Possess strong commitment to Chinese Medicine education with interests in pedagogical innovation and curriculum design.
  • Be proficient in both Chinese and English.
