Duties & Responsibilities

  • Assist the project leader in managing a digital mental health interventions research project.
  • Prepare project materials and manage project data.
  • Conduct data analysis and evidence reviews.
  • Facilitate public, patient, and carer involvement in research.
  • Write research protocols, proposals, and papers.
  • Liaise with collaborators to support project progression.
  • Prepare reports and presentation materials.
  • Perform additional duties as assigned by the Head of School or project leader.

Requirements & Qualifications

  • PhD or doctoral degree in Nursing, Social Work, Public Health, Rehabilitation, Psychology, Health Informatics or related field.
  • No more than five years of post-qualification experience at time of application.
  • Relevant research experience and experience writing research papers and proposals.
  • Proficiency in SPSS/R/Python, Excel, EndNote, NVivo, Microsoft Office, or web design/Chinese word processing.
  • Excellent written and spoken English.
  • Strong enthusiasm for research and self-motivation.
  • Good interpersonal and communication skills.
  • Ability to work independently and responsibly.
