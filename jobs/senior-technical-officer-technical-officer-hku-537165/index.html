Duties & Responsibilities

  • Maintain the School's prototyping workshop and equipment.
  • Support teaching, research, and prototyping activities for project-based courses.
  • Conduct workshops and assist students in developing innovative projects.
  • Provide technical support for the School's daily operations.

Requirements & Qualifications

  • Hold a relevant higher diploma or bachelor's degree.
  • Possess at least three years of relevant work experience (preferred for Senior Technical Officer).
  • Have extensive technical experience in at least two areas: robotics, electronics, embedded systems programming (C or Python), or 3D modeling software.
  • Demonstrate strong hands-on experience in developing rapid mechatronic prototypes.
  • Have experience operating and maintaining 3D printers, laser cutters, and basic electronic equipment.
  • Be self-motivated, able to work independently, and fluent in written and spoken Chinese and English.

Appointment

  • Full-time, two-year fixed-term contract with possibility of renewal; includes contract-end gratuity and University contribution to retirement benefits scheme (up to 10% of basic salary).
