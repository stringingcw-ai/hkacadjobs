Senior Lecturer / Lecturer (Music Technology)

Technological and Higher Education Institute of Hong Kong

Rank

Position type
Full-time
Reference
DDIT/SL_L(MT)/06/26

Duties & Responsibilities

  • Deliver lectures and practical sessions on music technology, including professional software like Unity, Unreal Engine, Wwise, and FMOD.
  • Teach AI applications in music creation, sound design, and production workflows.
  • Instruct students in advanced audio processing and immersive audio technologies such as Dolby Atmos.
  • Develop and update curriculum and assessment materials aligned with industry trends.
  • Support student learning through project supervision and portfolio guidance.
  • Participate in academic activities and provide administrative support for the programme.

Requirements & Qualifications

  • Hold a Master's degree (preferably Doctoral) in Sound/Music Technology or related fields with professional standing.
  • Have at least 3 years of tertiary-level teaching experience or equivalent industry experience.
  • Demonstrate proficiency in music production software including Unity, Unreal Engine, Wwise, FMOD, and AI music tools.
  • Possess advanced knowledge of sound engineering and immersive audio formats like Dolby Atmos.
  • Have practical coding/programming experience in music technology environments such as Unity, Unreal, Max/MSP, or Pure Data.
  • Maintain up-to-date knowledge of industry trends in music technology and creative industries.
  • Demonstrate strong interpersonal skills and fluency in English and Putonghua.
  • Meet specified Putonghua language proficiency criteria.
