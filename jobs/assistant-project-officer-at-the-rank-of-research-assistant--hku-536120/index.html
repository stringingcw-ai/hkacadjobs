Duties & Responsibilities

  • Assist with administration and research matters for a healthcare communication research initiative.
  • Recruit research participants and promote the project to stakeholders.
  • Conduct data collection, literature searches, and Chinese-to-English translation.
  • Create publicity materials including infographics, posters, and videos.
  • Manage the research group's website and social media channels.
  • Organize public lectures, academic conferences, and symposiums.

Requirements & Qualifications

  • Bachelor's degree or above in English Language and Linguistics, Language and Communication, Linguistics, or a related discipline.
  • Minimum graduation GPA of 3.3/4.3 from a reputable university.
  • Preferably bilingual in Cantonese-English or Mandarin-English.
  • Excellent analytical, interpersonal, and organizational skills.
  • Ability to work independently and collaboratively in multidisciplinary teams.
  • Experience assisting in externally funded research projects is preferred.

Appointment

  • Contract — Nine months with possibility of renewal subject to satisfactory performance and funding availability.

  • Application deadline: August 31, 2026 (or until the post is filled, whichever is earlier).
  • Commencement date: As soon as possible.
