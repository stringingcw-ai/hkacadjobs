Duties & Responsibilities

  • Design, synthesise and evaluate novel peptide analogues and biodegradable hydrogels for brain-targeted therapy.
  • Conduct peptide chemistry, nanomaterial/hydrogel engineering, and nose-to-brain delivery experiments.
  • Perform analytical characterisation using HPLC, LC-MS, NMR, gel permeation chromatography and surface plasmon resonance.
  • Design, cross-link and characterise polymer-based nanomaterials and hydrogels.
  • Conduct animal studies including dosing, sample collection, pharmacokinetic sampling and behavioural assessment.
  • Prepare study protocols, reports and contribute to manuscript writing.
  • Supervise and mentor undergraduate and postgraduate students.
  • Disseminate findings through peer-reviewed publications and conference presentations.

Requirements & Qualifications

  • Ph.D. in Pharmaceutical Sciences, Chemistry, Biochemistry, Bioengineering, Molecular Biology or related field.
  • Strong biological skills with proven track record in peptide or nanomaterial-based therapeutics research.
  • Hands-on expertise in nanomaterials and hydrogel formulation, characterisation and optimisation.
  • Experience in peptide synthesis and bioconjugation chemistry.
  • Proficiency in cell and molecular biology techniques including ELISA, Western blotting, immunohistochemistry and cell culture.
  • Experience with in vivo rodent models, pharmacokinetic sampling and behavioural testing.
  • Excellent written and spoken English with strong scientific communication skills.
  • Ability to work independently with excellent problem-solving skills and capacity to supervise students.

Appointment

  • Temporary, 1-year contract (renewable subject to funding and project progress)
