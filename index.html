About HKAcadJobs

Every university opening in Hong Kong, in one place.

From the creator

Hi, I'm Cedric — welcome to HKAcadJobs! This all started from a complaint from my partner (he doesn't complain often, so when he does, you have to take it seriously) about how difficult the job hunting process is for academic roles in Hong Kong: no consolidated view, every opening is hosted in silos across each university's career portal, which he had to check across 10+ sites every single day.

I'm not a tech guy, but I started putting my thoughts to Claude, which helped me build this initial version of HKAcadJobs from scratch — it runs a daily scrape to capture all university openings in Hong Kong into a single list, with a brief job description summarised by AI.

There's still a lot to improve and optimise, but I hope you find it useful for now! For any bugs or suggestions, feel free to reach out via the feedback form at the bottom of the website — I'll try to keep up with messages during off-work hours. You can also see the changes I make every day via the change log on GitHub.

What is this?

HKAcadJobs is a free, independent job board that aggregates academic and university positions from 13 Hong Kong institutions. It is updated daily and requires no login or subscription.

Institutions covered

  • University of Hong Kong (HKU)
  • Chinese University of Hong Kong (CUHK)
  • HK University of Science & Technology (HKUST)
  • Hong Kong Polytechnic University (PolyU)
  • City University of Hong Kong (CityU)
  • Hong Kong Baptist University (HKBU)
  • Lingnan University (LU)
  • Education University of Hong Kong (EdUHK)
  • Hong Kong Metropolitan University (HKMU)
  • Hang Seng University of Hong Kong (HSU)
  • Saint Francis University (SFU)
  • Hong Kong Shue Yan University (HKSYU)
  • Vocational Training Council (VTC)
  • HKU School of Professional and Continuing Education (HKU SPACE)
  • HKCC / SPEED — College of Professional and Continuing Education (CPCE)
  • Hong Kong Chu Hai College (HKCHC)
  • Technological and Higher Education Institute of Hong Kong (THEi)

How it works

A Python scraper runs daily via GitHub Actions, pulling job listings directly from each university's official career portal. AI-generated summaries (via Claude) provide a quick overview of each role. All data is stored in a CSV file and served as a static site with no backend.

How to use

  • Search by keyword, then filter by institution (multi-select), academic area, rank (multi-select), or role type
  • Click any row to open the full job detail panel
  • Star a position to save it for later — saved locally in your browser
  • Apply filters and click 🔖 Save filter to bookmark a search — access it anytime from the Saved tab
  • Click the Saved button in the top bar to view your saved positions and saved filters

This site is not affiliated with any Hong Kong university. Data is sourced from official public career portals. AI summaries are generated automatically and may contain inaccuracies — always refer to the official university career portal for the most accurate information.
