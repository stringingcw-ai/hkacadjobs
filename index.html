About HKAcadJobs

Every university opening in Hong Kong, in one place.

From the creator

Hi, I'm Cedric — welcome to HKAcadJobs! This all started from a complaint from my partner (he doesn't complain often, so when he does, you have to take it seriously) about how difficult the job hunting process is for academic roles in Hong Kong: no consolidated view, every opening is hosted in silos across each university's career portal, which he had to check across 10+ sites every single day.

I'm not a tech guy, but I started putting my thoughts to Claude, which helped me build this initial version of HKAcadJobs from scratch — it runs a daily scrape to capture all university openings in Hong Kong into a single list, with a brief job description summarised by AI.

There's still a lot to improve and optimise, but I hope you find it useful for now! For any bugs or suggestions, feel free to reach out via the feedback form at the bottom of the website — I'll try to keep up with messages during off-work hours. You can also see the changes I make every day via the change log on GitHub.

What is this?

HKAcadJobs is a free, independent job board that aggregates academic and university positions from 17 Hong Kong institutions. It is updated daily, and browsing needs no login or subscription.

How it works

A Python scraper runs daily via GitHub Actions, pulling job listings directly from each university's official career portal. AI-generated summaries (via Claude) provide a quick overview of each role. The listings are stored in a CSV file and served as a static site. Accounts, saved searches and job alerts use Supabase.

With a free account, you can also add your CV and we'll find the open positions that fit you best. Claude reads your CV and compares your experience with every open position, then explains each match.

How to use

  • Search by keyword, then filter by institution (multi-select), academic area, rank (multi-select), or role type
  • Click any row to open the full job detail panel
  • Star a position to save it for later — saved locally in your browser
  • Apply filters and click 🔖 Save filter to bookmark a search — access it anytime from the Saved tab
  • Click the Saved button in the top bar to view your saved positions and saved filters
  • Click ✨ Find jobs that fit your CV to see the positions that best fit your experience. You can also get an email when a new one appears

Privacy

You can browse without an account. We use Google Analytics to count visits and clicks. It doesn't get your name or email address.

If you create an account, we keep your email address and what you save (positions, filters and alerts) in our database, which runs on Supabase. Alert emails are sent through Resend.

CV matching. When you use "Find jobs that fit your CV":

  • Your file is read in your browser. Email addresses, phone numbers, ID numbers and details such as date of birth are removed before anything is sent.
  • The rest of the text, or the address of the web page you give us, goes to our matching service. There it is analysed by Claude, an AI model from Anthropic, which describes your experience and compares it with the open positions. Anthropic processes it for us and doesn't use it to train its models.
  • We don't store your CV or its text. For the daily usage limits we record the date, size and cost of each request against your account (not what it contained), and delete these records after 90 days.
  • Your latest profile and matches are kept in your browser so you can come back to them. Signing out clears them.
  • If you choose to save your profile for match alerts, we keep it with your email address. It records your fields, specialisms and experience level, never your name or contact details. You can delete it any time under My CV profile.

To see, correct or delete your data, or with any privacy question, contact us through the feedback form.
